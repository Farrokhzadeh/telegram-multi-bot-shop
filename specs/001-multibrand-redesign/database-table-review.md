# Database Table Review — Strict Relational / No JSON Columns

**Revised**: 2026-10-29 | **Status**: OWNER-REVIEW CANDIDATE, not executable DDL or migration permission.
**Database name candidate**: `commerce_core` (brand neutral).
**Approved baseline**: **27 physical tables** (the prior 23-table base plus owner-approved `discount_revisions`, `bot_activity_events`, `supplier_request_attempts` and `product_supplier_sources`) for the **owner-confirmed private-DM-only** customer bots, subject to verifying that legacy order history can safely omit `order_items`. The owner has explicitly removed the proposed historical `exchange_rates` table; the *current* source rate/freshness state lives in typed `brands` columns and each accepted rate is snapshotted on `orders`. Customer group/topic sessions and toman-denominated customer wallets are **out of scope** for v1. Legacy multi-product history may require one additional archival-line table.

**Additional review**: [Full redundancy/normalization audit across the 27-table candidate](normalization-audit.md). The owner has approved the **USD-only wallet** and **private-DM-only customer chat** reductions, three additional typed history tables, and the approved supplier-source mapping relation. Other candidate removals still require specific approval. Wallet accounting is **USD**, while USDT remains an external quote/network unit.

## Design rule

The first release uses **zero `json`/`jsonb` columns, zero EAV/key-value configuration tables, and no untyped job payload column**. Store domain facts as typed scalar columns with `NOT NULL`, foreign keys, `CHECK` constraints and explicit indexes. Rare raw provider evidence may live in separately protected immutable object storage, referenced by asset identifier and digest; it must never be the sole source of a money, stock, or reconciliation decision. Ciphertext uses `bytea` plus a key identifier, not a back door for mutable business-state JSON.

Add a column through a reviewed migration when an actual invariant needs it. A relational junction table is appropriate for a *real* many-to-many relationship; do not substitute encoded IDs or comma-separated strings.

### Common conventions

- IDs are globally unique where required. Each order also has an independent UTC `created_at timestamptz`; do not infer authoritative time from its identifier.
- Telegram IDs use `bigint`; amounts use `numeric(p,s)` and a constrained currency code, never floating point. Precision, scale and rounding await approved source/provider inventory.
- `users.id` is a brand-membership ID, **not** a cross-brand global customer profile; `UNIQUE (brand_id,telegram_id)` prevents duplicate membership. Customer-owned rows may refer to `user_id` alone; database-enforced brand-match guards (typed FK where possible, trigger otherwise) must prohibit an order/product/coupon/campaign from joining distinct brands. Brand-aware work such as broadcasts still retains its own `brand_id` and checked `(brand_id,user_id)` FK.
- Encrypted sensitive fields have `*_ciphertext bytea`, `*_key_id` and scope/entity/row/field authenticated binding. **Brand-owned secrets use per-brand keys; unsold shared `inventory_items` credentials use an independent platform-inventory key.** Decryption keys remain outside the DB and the inventory key is unavailable to ordinary bot consumers.
- Finite states are checked and transition-tested. Audit records are append-only. No public customer endpoint accepts an untrusted `brand_id` selector.

**Important limit**: Telegram has no universal outgoing-message idempotency token. Crash-after-send-before-message-ID-recording can produce a duplicate visible message. Promise exactly-once *local money/stock effects* and durable remote retry evidence, not impossible guaranteed exactly-once Telegram delivery.

**Campaign FK correction for final DDL**: Since there may be several `campaigns` rows for one `(batch_id,brand_id)` (one per language), that pair cannot itself be a foreign-key target. Use a unique composite `(campaign_id,batch_id,brand_id)` target tied to the campaign's `id`, or equivalent database-checked constraint, when a `telegram_deliveries` row stores `campaign_id` plus `campaign_batch_id` and `brand_id`. Recipient uniqueness across campaign languages still needs `(campaign_batch_id,user_id)`.

**Expiry/cancellation edit race guard**: A queued Telegram edit MUST re-read `orders.payment_status` immediately before editing: show **Failed/Expired** only while status is `expired`, or **Cancelled — replaced by new order** only while status is `cancelled`. Stale edits must not overwrite a newly paid/reconciled status or confuse the replacement's Telegram message.

**30-minute external-payment deadline**: The bot prominently displays the exact `payment_due_at` and the 30-minute validity limit before initiating bank/crypto payment and in the order's payment message. `payment_due_at = created_at + interval '30 minutes'` for each newly created **unpaid** external-payment order; wallet-funded and 100%-free instant purchases do not need a timer. A durable scheduled `jobs(job_kind='expire_payment', order_id, available_at=payment_due_at)` performs a conditional payment-state transition from `awaiting_payment` to `expired` and queues `telegram_deliveries(action='edit',order_id,target_message_id=...)` to change the original message to "Failed / Payment window expired". Edit errors are retried, and the next interaction displays the authoritative state. A bank receipt **submitted before the deadline** changes to `under_review` (admin approval may occur much later); verified/on-chain evidence **observed before the deadline** moves crypto to `confirmation_pending` while confirmations finish. A paid, under-review, pending-confirmation or restock-pending obligation **must never** be timed out merely because 30 minutes passed. Late-real-money events after expiry are recorded and reconciled; never discard funds or silently reactivate an expired order. Fully verified full-value late USDT is credited once to the original brand's USD wallet; late bank/provider anomalies remain manually reconciled.

**Payment method locked; one actively unpaid order per membership (owner confirmed)**: Once created, `orders.payment_method` and `orders.currency` never change. A customer creating another valid order within the **same brand membership** atomically marks the previous `awaiting_payment` order `cancelled` and releases its coupon claim, then issues a new immutable quote and 30-minute deadline. An invalid replacement does not cancel the old order. A partial unique index on `orders(user_id)` for `payment_status='awaiting_payment'`, together with a per-membership lock, prevents two new pending orders from surviving concurrent bot updates. A receipt already submitted (`under_review`), qualified crypto evidence (`confirmation_pending`), or paid order is **not replaceable**, even though money may not yet be fully settled. Old provider references and receipt evidence remain for late-money reconciliation; never silently redirect payments to the new order.

**Wallet funding origin resolved (owner confirmed)**: The same `payments` table holds external order payments and **crypto-only USD wallet top-ups**, using exactly one of `order_id` or `topup_user_id`. The latter references `users.id` directly so no wallet row must exist when a funding attempt begins; successful deduplicated settlement creates/credits the correct USD wallet subject to the approved topup-eligibility policy. Bank/card-to-card top-ups are prohibited. **New crypto invoices accept only `network_asset='USDTTRC20'` (TRON/TRC20) or `'USDTBSC'` (BNB Smart Chain/BEP20)**; `TRX` is not a permitted coin, and generic `USDT` without a network is insufficient. The actual provider `pay_currency` is compared with the invoice's frozen selected currency, and asset/network mismatches fail verification. `kind`/`currency` can be considered for removal only after legacy migrations establish whether they contain independent evidence not derivable from immutable order method, origin or USD topup rule; keep network asset, network quantity, provider settlement and actual money amounts regardless. **No new physical table** is needed.

**Single NOWPayments account for all three brands (owner confirmed)**: Keep one shared merchant credential/key set in restricted service configuration outside this database, not three copies on `brands` and not a new `payment_provider_accounts` table. A payment's stored unique provider identity and exclusive `order_id`/`topup_user_id` association give the authoritative brand via `orders.user_id → users.brand_id` or `payments.topup_user_id → users.brand_id`. The callback must never trust an external brand label as its tenant identity. Use internal globally unique invoice/payment IDs, keep each brand's payment message and order description independently branded, and audit provider-hosted invoice/receipt metadata before cutover for possible disclosure of common merchant ownership. Provider credential compromise affects all brands; operational rotation, least privilege and verification need platform-level treatment.

**Separate bank cards, newest saved replaces old; no added table (owner confirmed)**: The two Iranian brands have different active card numbers. **Exactly one card is active per brand; the last successfully committed card replacement immediately becomes that brand's sole destination for newly created orders.** Store each brand's active receiving number/holder under its **brand encryption key** in typed `brands` fields; keep a **platform-keyed** fingerprint (same keyed normalization across brands, not a per-brand digest) only to detect accidentally reused card numbers. Use version-checked atomic admin updates to prevent concurrent stale saves from silently winning. Freeze the receiving card/holder in encrypted, immutable bank-payment `payments` snapshots for historic receipt review; **decrypt then re-encrypt each snapshot under its own payment-row/field authenticated context**, never copy the original `brands` ciphertext bytes into a different row whose authenticated data differs. Old pending orders keep their original destination and 30-minute deadline even after the current brand card changes. If the owner later requests several simultaneous cards per brand, a real relational `brand_bank_cards` table would become justified. Customers may still identify the same legal holder through their banking-app transfer preview; separate PANs alone cannot prevent that.

**Confirmed owner behavior**: A bulk buyer who prepays for 100 future units receives **one 100%-off code with 100 unit uses**, not 100 separately generated codes; 1/5/10/etc. units can redeem across separate later orders until the entitlement is exhausted. Customer goodwill/replacement can use a 100% coupon, and campaigns need 10%, 50% or 100% codes. Codes are **not** equivalent to cash refunds or the previously agreed automatic crypto-to-USD-wallet compensation. **Prepaid codes are account-bound to one `users.id` membership within one brand** and must reference that buyer's original paid liability and product/quantity entitlement; no transfer or cross-brand redemption, even when a code string is shared. Unused paid units must remain traceable. Marketing codes may be product-wide with usage caps. T029 still inventories exact legacy behavior, rounding, rate staleness and topup restrictions before DDL approval.

**Automatic coupon behavior; external claims approved**: Keep the existing product/global applicability, assigned-user restriction and automatic **best eligible** discount selection for *both* USDT and toman. A chosen discount must cover **all requested units**: if only 10 *unheld* uses remain and a customer enters 11, **invalidate that quantity**, display a maximum of 10 and ask for a corrected quantity; **do not create a failed order** or silently buy 10/charge the 11th. When an external-payment order is successfully created, insert `discount_uses(status='held')` under a lock on `discounts`, ensuring **new claims** fit `max(0,max_total_uses - max(0,redeemed_count + usage_adjustment_units) - SUM(held.units_covered))`, including any edited per-user cap; previously valid holds may exceed a newly reduced admin cap and remain protected. The 30-minute expiry or successful replacement cancellation changes the claim to `released` atomically; on-time receipt/provider evidence and paid-but-unfulfilled bank obligations keep the hold past 30 minutes. On committed successful fulfillment, transition to `redeemed` and increment `discounts.redeemed_count` in the same serialized transaction. **If verified crypto payment finds no stock, compensate the full frozen USD-valued amount and release the coupon hold in one atomic transaction: no item delivered, no coupon redemption.** Instant/free checkout redeems only with successful stock allocation. **There is still no advance stock reservation.** **Approved option A**: returning a campaign-code use never extends `discounts.ends_at`, reactivates an expired promotion or auto-issues a replacement. Valid holds created before coupon expiry remain usable **for the original protected order**, even if approval/delivery runs after expiry. Prepaid 100%-off future-unit entitlements default to `ends_at IS NULL` and remain traceable paid obligations.

**Coupon security and reporting**: Random codes are generated in a protected admin workflow; persist a brand-key encrypted value plus a keyed lookup digest, never plaintext. Allow authorized customer “My Discounts” and notification flows to decrypt only eligible codes. Rate-limit attempts to guess public marketing codes. Outstanding *prepaid* code counts/value must remain queryable by source paid order; coupon expiration/revocation must not silently erase outstanding prepaid liabilities.

## Boundaries that should **not** be merged

- `wallets` / `wallet_entries`: current balance vs immutable financial accounting.
- `orders` / `payments`: one order can have many settlement attempts.
- `payments` / `fulfillments`: money received is not a delivered product.
- `products` / `brand_products`: base inheritance vs per-brand overrides and price.
- `fulfillments` / `supplier_requests`: remote uncertainty requires its own reconciliation identity.
- `support_tickets` / `support_messages`: messages are an append-only thread.
- `jobs` / `telegram_deliveries`: finishing a job is not proof Telegram accepted a send.

## End-to-end relational flows

1. **Customer**: authenticated bot token establishes brand → look up `users(brand_id,telegram_id)` → all reads/writes scope to that brand.
2. **Wallet checkout**: `brand_products` quote → automatically choose one eligible discount; if entered quantity exceeds the available uses, show the maximum and prompt again **before making an order** → lock coupon, wallet if net amount positive, and required `inventory_items` → create one `orders` row and `fulfillments` per unit, optional `wallet_entries` debit, allocate stock, commit `discount_uses` and queue delivery in one transaction. Concurrency races retry quantity selection without a debit or order.
3. **Immediate (non-preorder) crypto paid without stock**: verify and dedupe `payments` → credit the frozen confirmed checkout **USD-valued amount** to this brand's **USD wallet** with one `wallet_entries` entry → preserve order status → notify via `telegram_deliveries`. For a USDT quote, the wallet receives the same numeric amount in USD units under the specified internal policy; exact requested amount is in `payments.amount_due`, immutable quote data in `orders`.
4. **Bank accepted without stock**: approved `payments` and paid/unfulfilled `orders` stay pending indefinitely, subject only to audited staff resolution.
5. **Supplier unknown/incomplete acquisition**: one order-level `supplier_requests` batch owns per-unit encrypted acquisition evidence and attempt history. Three of five acquired items remain internal and release zero customer items; the full paid order waits for admin review and a complete manifest. No automatic new purchase, credit or external refund. Per-unit evidence never grants separate customer release.
6. **Broadcast**: create `campaigns` per brand/language sharing `batch_id` → materialize one `telegram_deliveries` per eligible **brand membership `user_id`** and batch (correct localized row selected) → throttle per bot and store remote message ID.
7. **Unpaid invoice deadline**: store `orders.payment_due_at` and payment message ID → background conditional expiry at 30 minutes → edit original message to failed/expired; record late receipts/transfers for manual reconciliation, never erase confirmed payment.
8. **Migration**: `legacy_id_map` preserves source and target identities, one posting opening ledger entry establishes frozen wallet balance, and historical entries import as non-posting evidence.
