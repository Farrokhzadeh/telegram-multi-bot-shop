# Supplier failure and reconciliation policy

## Accepted obligations

A supplier request belongs to the original order/member, exact selected source/account/product/variant, positive remote remainder and immutable acceptance cost/currency/quote. A later source selection, retail price, stock arrival, cost refresh or retry never rebinds those facts. Local allocations already acquired for a mixed order stay internal until the complete order is authorized. An accepted zero-due order is an obligation with a held coupon, without a fabricated payment, invoice, wallet debit or expiry timer.

## Before purchase

Persist and commit the attempt before the remote call. Recheck exact bound identity, ready-item eligibility, current source validity, fresh authoritative same-currency unit cost and an enforceable maximum unit charge. The automatic maximum is the original acceptance unit cost multiplied by 1.10, inclusive. Multiply that fixed unit maximum by the immutable remote quantity when validating total charge; the provider contract must cover all charge components, including fees. A later quote cannot reset the baseline. An explicit current-admin cost approval may set a separate audited ceiling for the exact blocked request, before the first submit or after every prior submit is positively proven no-effect. A post-attempt approval creates no purchase work; another explicit safe-retry decision is required. The approval it changes neither original customer terms nor original cost evidence. An uncapped catalog-read-then-purchase sequence is blocked.

## Uncertain or partial result

Archive protected original response evidence before committing item attribution. Timeouts, malformed or lost responses, archive failure and generic HTTP error codes do not prove no effect. Started work is never automatically submitted again. Expired started leases become unknown and enqueue read-only lookup against the original purchase or a proven intent lookup. A lease that expired before a durable attempt may reclaim the same local job; no provider call is allowed before that attempt commits. Delayed known completion through a matching lookup clears only the transient provider-unknown review; partial, identity, cost or credential-recovery reviews remain protected.

Original immutable attempts remain evidence. A later status lookup points to the original submit rather than rewriting its outcome. Confirmed-no-effect requires an explicit trusted result, protected evidence and no acquired customer units or charge. Partial customer units stay encrypted internally and preserve the whole held coupon; no smaller manifest, retry of guessed missing units, automatic compensation or local-stock rebinding is allowed. Staff alerts carry metadata only to the configured private staff chat.

Stable provider/account item identities are checked across customer fulfillments and admin-held inventory. Duplicate/reordered recovery must preserve prior content and disposition. Explicitly verified extras are protected admin bonuses, never extra customer units, automatically saleable stock or a reason to purchase more. Ambiguous extras retain evidence for review. Protected bonus access requires a current independent administrator session and a durable audit; group membership and ordinary customer APIs grant no access.

## Explicit decisions after confirmed failure

Only an independently authenticated administrator may choose a content-checked, revision-bound retry or terminal same-brand wallet credit. Retry requires every earlier submit to be proven no-effect, either directly or by a matching later status lookup. It preserves original binding, unmet quantity and nonrolling baseline/explicit approved ceiling, carries the exact retry authorization and commits before the next call. Neither local intent IDs nor request headers establish provider idempotency.

Credit serializes against request/worker/allocation/manifest state. Wallet and crypto orders use the original frozen discounted net USD; bank orders require an explicit positive administrator-confirmed USD amount and reason, without inferred FX. Zero-due orders cannot generate fictitious cash. Unknown effects, leased procurement or acquired remote units block terminal credit. The single typed USD ledger posting, administrator audit, terminal resolution, admin retention of local acquired goods and full still-held coupon release commit together. Replay creates no additional posting, release or audit; changed input conflicts. Coupon terms and expiry are unchanged, and redeemed claims are never released. Original payment history remains paid; no external refund, repayment, payout execution or payout record is inferred.

## Qualification boundary

Only synthetic adapters, fixtures and disposable databases are authorized. The provider-neutral implementation cannot qualify any real provider's current currency, remote idempotency, ready-item eligibility, enforceable ceiling, item identities or credential recovery. T057 stays gated per provider until sanitized authoritative contract/capability evidence exists. Normal hosts remain fail closed, without live supplier transports or scheduling. No production/staging migration, source credentials/data, live supplier/payment/Telegram/FX request or deployment is authorized by this policy.
