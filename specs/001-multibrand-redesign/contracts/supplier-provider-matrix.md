# Supplier capability matrix and local-preorder reconciliation

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-29. **Scope:** specification/source review only.
**Evidence:** staging commit `5752ec9c21e49b93e1d7d9f0b95bb8a7d713737d`; no supplier calls, credential access, deployed-image inspection, database queries or live purchase validation in this review.

## Owner-approved behavior

1. A shop product may have several supplier mappings; an administrator selects the active source. No automatic supplier selection, fallback or multi-provider split procurement; approved local-first mixing uses the one selected supplier for the remainder (FR-078).
2. Local stock allocates automatically oldest paid order first only when the complete requested quantity exists; no partial assignment/reservation or smaller-order bypass.
3. Supplier 3-of-5 retains three encrypted internal acquired items but releases zero customer content; the whole paid order requires administrator review. Release only a complete verified set after review clearance.
4. All external refunds and their recordkeeping remain outside AccAbad.

FR-064/065 and SC-033/034 capture the new behavior. The approved physical baseline now contains **28 tables**, including owner-approved `product_supplier_sources` below. Final constraints/configuration remain reviewed before DDL.

## First-release eligible product scope — owner approved (2026-10-30)

Automated supplier procurement supports ready-to-deliver accounts, keys and codes only, with no customer-provided fulfillment inputs. Exclude email activation, buyer-email slot assignment, existing-account upgrades and manual/custom supplier delivery. Normal customer redemption of an already delivered code remains allowed. Ready-to-deliver is a product/input boundary, not a synchronous API-success guarantee; processing/status/export and one-time encrypted content recovery remain necessary.

Validate exact external product/variant requirements at mapping enablement/publication and before purchase. Unknown/unverified requirements fail eligibility. Block known excluded items before payable checkout; a requirement change after accepted payment preserves the original source and full paid obligation for administrator review under FR-066, with no fallback purchase/automatic compensation. Full-order-only release remains FR-067. No new customer-input/activation table, form or generic payload is approved; local/manual scope is unchanged. FR-068 / SC-037.

## Owner-approved supplier-source relation — table 27 (final constraints pending)

The owner approved `product_supplier_sources` as table 27, with candidate fields:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id`, `product_id` | Stable mapping identity and shared shop product |
| `provider_code` | Resolves the provider's one configured account across products/brands; no separate account selector or credential copy on mapping rows |
| `external_product_id`, nullable `external_variant_id` | Provider-native identities as separate typed values |
| `enabled`, `revision`, timestamps | Availability, optimistic concurrency and lifecycle |

The selected schema uses null-aware partial unique identity indexes and only products.active_supplier_source_id, with a same-product composite FK. Selecting a pointer requires enabled source and actor/reason/revision audit. A later disable retains the selected identity but blocks remote eligibility; it must not be rejected by an always-enabled FK/check. There is no second selected flag.

**Selection scope — owner approved (2026-10-29):** one shared active source per master product, affecting all three brands together. There is no per-brand supplier-source override. The sole pointer belongs to products, not brand_products. Check same product and enabled status when selecting; keep revision/audit guards and the allowed selected-disable lifecycle. Previously bound requests retain their original source identity after a switch; newly bound requests from every brand use the new shared selection. Binding is now owner-approved at accepted payment as described below.

**T094 offline selection command:** `SupplierSourceSelectionStore.SelectAsync` requires an independent current administrator, product/source IDs, expected product/source revisions and a content-bound command ID. Authentication/audit precedes product then source row locks. Selection requires supplier fulfillment, the exact product revision and a same-product enabled source at the exact source revision. It advances only the product revision/pointer and atomically queues bounded restock evaluation. A committed replay returns the original resulting revision without restoring a subsequently changed pointer; changed command content fails. Stale/foreign/disabled selection rolls its audit and work back. Selection does not attest ready-item eligibility or provider capability: existing direct-refresh/acceptance/procurement checks remain mandatory. No source mapping creation, enablement, credential configuration, new HTTP route or existing-environment DDL is introduced by this checkpoint.

**T094 mapping lifecycle continuation:** the same store creates immutable disabled mappings and reads bounded authenticated metadata pages. Creation preserves the canonical provider/product/nullable-variant uniqueness; replacing an identity means creating another mapping, never editing referenced identity/history. Enabling authenticates and checks the source revision, releases all database locks, obtains an exact-product/variant ready-item observation from the existing CatalogStore configured-account boundary, then authenticates again and rechecks product/source revisions under product/source locks. Ready out-of-stock products may be configured but remain non-purchasable. Unknown/ineligible/input-collecting/malformed/unavailable observations fail closed. A fresh monotonic 30-second bound applies through the final write; enabling and its new-revision typed observation/audit/restock work commit together. The temporary preflight audit is rolled back before transport; committed command replay skips transport and never reapplies state. Disabling preserves the selected pointer and original accepted obligations; re-enabling cannot clear paid review or enqueue another purchase. Full-local checkout retains its supplier-refresh exemption. No credentials, account pools, new HTTP endpoints, tables or DDL are introduced; real provider/account configuration and capability evidence remain independently gated.

**Account cardinality — owner approved (2026-10-29):** one configured account per supplier is enough, shared across its product mappings and all three brands. No account pools, per-brand accounts or separately selectable account per mapping. Recommend a typed protected provider configuration/secret reference resolved by `provider_code`; the approved mapping does not include a separately selectable `supplier_connection_ref`. Exact config persistence remains under review; this does not approve another account table.

Paid requests still retain the actual original account identity. Same-account key rotation must not change that identity or resend a purchase. Replacing the configured account cannot silently redirect reconciliation of old requests; unavailable original-account access or identity mismatch needs administrator review. Never store tokens in mapping rows, request snapshots, URLs, logs or evidence summaries.

**Protected configuration implementation contract:** `SupplierCredentialFiles` loads a separate read-only version-1 manifest with exactly `schemaVersion`, `deploymentId`, `revision`, `service` and `accounts`. Every account has exactly `provider`, `accountRef`, `credentialFile` and `credentialSha256`. An independent `SupplierCredentialGrant` pins deployment, positive credential-configuration revision, Api/JobHost service and the complete provider/account set; BotHost and duplicate provider accounts are rejected. Each nonempty credential is at most 8192 opaque bytes, read through the existing Linux descriptor-relative no-follow/read-only reader, and checked against its 32-byte SHA-256 manifest commitment before publication. Unknown/duplicate/missing fields, incomplete grants, reused file references, unsafe files and mixed credential versions fail closed with sanitized errors. Credentials stay in one disposable immutable snapshot per service, accessed only through trusted synchronous callbacks; metadata contains provider/account references only. Dispose clears credential buffers, and failed loads clear already-read material. Publish immutable credential files and replace the whole manifest atomically; there is no watcher, environment fallback or partial configuration update. Credential revisions are independent of commerce keyring revisions, but protected CatalogStore composition requires the same deployment and service. Same-account reloads preserve logical account identity and accepted source snapshots; different-account reconciliation remains blocked. Configuration JSON is outside PostgreSQL; no table/column, account pool, provider SDK, live adapter or normal-host activation is introduced. Generated-file/native probes do not qualify real provider identity or deployed key custody under E2/E4.

**Binding time — owner approved (2026-10-29):** freeze the current shared source at accepted payment: committed wallet debit, verified crypto accepted for the live order, or administrator-approved bank payment. Unpaid order creation, invoice opening and unapproved receipts do not bind. In one local transaction, serialize source selection against admin edits and commit accepted payment, immutable account/provider/product/variant identity and durable procurement work. No supplier network call occurs inside that transaction. Customer quote terms stay fixed. Replay or a later admin switch never redirects an accepted request.

**Unavailable selected source — owner-approved default:** if the source is disabled, missing or known unable to supply at acceptance, retain accepted payment and a visible paid/unfulfilled administrator-review obligation. Retain the selected identity where known and a typed blocker; do not fabricate usable mapping or runnable procurement when absent. Re-enabling/replacing the shared source does not automatically clear that review gate. A source becoming unavailable after binding also retains its original request identity and paid liability. No silent fallback, repeat purchase, wallet credit or external refund. Known checkout invalidity may be rejected before payment is accepted; the rule never authorizes discarding money already accepted. Existing expired/replaced late-crypto recovery remains wallet recovery, without supplier purchasing. Exact typed blocker/snapshot storage and zero-due acceptance fields remain pre-DDL design review items; FR-073 resolves automatic coupon-covered supplier procurement.

## Fully coupon-covered supplier checkout — owner approved (2026-10-30)

When a valid automatically selected 100%-off coupon covers every requested unit of an eligible supplier product, checkout automatically accepts the zero-due order and schedules purchase from the selected supplier without a separate administrator approval. Revalidate membership/brand/product eligibility, whole-quantity coupon capacity and the selected eligible source in one serialized local checkout transaction; commit the full coupon claim as `held`, `no_payment_required` acceptance, immutable source/account/product/variant revision and durable supplier procurement work together. This committed acceptance is the source-binding point for zero-due supplier orders. No customer wallet balance is required, no wallet debit/credit or fabricated external payment/invoice is created, and no unpaid-payment timer applies. Call the supplier only after commit using the original request/intent and existing provider-specific uncertainty rules. Hold the whole coupon claim while procurement is pending/unknown/incomplete; redeem it exactly once with complete-order fulfillment, not merely on checkout or supplier submission. Invalid coupon/quantity/source before commit leaves no accepted order, hold or procurement work. A source/eligibility failure discovered after acceptance retains the accepted order, held entitlement and original binding in administrator review without fallback, automatic repurchase or compensation; zero-due acceptance is not an actual monetary payment. Existing full-order delivery, verified-admin-only bonus routing, owner coupon-edit rights and local/immediate-sale rules remain unchanged.

This applies to verified eligible ready-to-deliver supplier mappings only. It does not waive FR-068 pre-purchase eligibility, FR-066 retained-obligation review after acceptance, provider idempotency proof or FR-067 complete-order release. Zero-due binding occurs at committed checkout, while positive-due supplier orders still bind at their accepted wallet/crypto/bank payment. No live supplier offer/purchase is established by this decision.
