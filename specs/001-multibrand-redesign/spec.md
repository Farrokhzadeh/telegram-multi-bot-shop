# Feature Specification: AccAbad Multi-Brand Commerce Redesign

**Feature identifier**: `001-multibrand-redesign`  
**Created**: 2026-10-26  
**Status**: Draft — backend-only delivery scope; supplier selection and local-preorder FIFO approved; remaining schema/policy and provider evidence gates open  
**Input**: Architecture clarification with the project owner, October 2026. This is the specification for a **new repository**, not an instruction to merge or deploy the historical Native AOT feature branch.

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 — Independent branded customer experience (P1)

A Telegram customer can use any of the three stores without encountering customer-facing details belonging to another brand. A returning customer has independent settings, wallet balances, order history, and support flows in each brand.

**Why this priority**: Brand separation is a business invariant, not a visual preference.

**Independent Test**: The same Telegram ID joins all three bots, purchases from each and inspects every reachable menu, notification, order, error and support flow. No foreign-brand record or identity is exposed.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. Given a Telegram ID present in brands A and C, when the customer enters brand A, then only the brand A profile, balances, catalog, rules, support and purchases are visible.
2. Given the same ID has never started brand B, when an operator broadcasts from brand B, then that ID is not treated as an eligible brand B recipient.
3. Given the ID is blocked or unsubscribed in one brand, when another brand sends an eligible notification, then the other brand's state is independent.

### User Story 2 — Wallet purchase of the last stocked item (P1)

A customer can complete an immediate wallet-funded purchase only when both a sufficient brand-specific balance and allocatable stock exist. If two customers compete for the last item, precisely one receives it, and the other is rejected without a debit.

**Why this priority**: Prevents double spending, double allocation and losses across shared inventory.

**Independent Test**: Concurrently submit 50 valid wallet purchases against one available credential; verify exactly one successful allocation/debit/order and 49 unchanged wallet balances.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. Given one item and two eligible wallet buyers, when checkout is confirmed concurrently, then precisely one atomic allocation and debit succeeds, and the other receives an out-of-stock response.
2. Given a failed database operation, when checkout aborts, then allocation, order and wallet movement are all rolled back.
3. Given successful checkout but failed Telegram delivery, when delivery retries, then the order and debit are not repeated and the item is not sold again.

### User Story 3 — Confirm external payment when stock is unavailable (P1)

Customers who have already paid through crypto do not lose funds when stock runs out. Paid manual bank receipts remain an explicit unresolved fulfillment obligation if stock is unavailable at approval time.

**Why this priority**: Money received cannot be treated like an unpaid checkout rejection.

**Independent Test**: Replay the same paid callback several times after stock is exhausted and verify one payment record, one wallet credit, and zero deliveries. Separately approve a bank receipt against empty stock and verify a visible paid/unfulfilled case without an imaginary allocation.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. Given a verified crypto payment and no available local stock, when fulfillment is attempted, then the full eligible received amount is credited exactly once to that brand's wallet, the order is marked not fulfilled, and the customer is told that the remedy was wallet credit rather than an external blockchain refund.
2. Given a bank receipt approved after stock exhaustion, when staff confirms payment, then it remains paid and awaiting restock indefinitely until an administrator explicitly resolves it; it is neither fulfilled nor silently converted to wallet credit.
3. Given a duplicate or delayed provider callback, when processed, then it produces no duplicate ledger credit, delivery or order.

### User Story 4 — Centrally manage three independent brands (P1)

An authorized administrator can manage all three stores through one secure administrative backend interface, including brand configuration, bot identity, required channels, shared stock, product overrides, orders, payments, support and campaigns. A graphical dashboard is deferred to a future feature.

**Why this priority**: Replaces three independent administration surfaces while preserving distinct customer-facing brands.

**Independent Test**: An authorized admin API client can find each brand's records, edit a shared product and a brand override, approve a receipt, configure multiple required channels, and inspect corresponding audit entries.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. Given a base product with brand B name and icon overrides, when other base fields change, then B inherits the changes except its name and icon.
2. Given a broadcast targeting all three bots, when launched, then each eligible bot membership receives a separately branded message; a user in all three may receive three messages.
3. Given a brand-specific asset or message, when rendered from another brand, then it is denied or excluded.

### User Story 5 — Safe supplier fulfillment (P1)

An order depending on an external supplier is tracked independently from payment settlement. An uncertain supplier outcome is reconciled before retry or compensation can create a duplicate fulfillment.

**Independent Test**: Simulate supplier success, confirmed failure, timeout-after-success and incomplete five-unit acquisition (three verified outputs). Prove every real submit/status/export call is recorded once, incomplete content remains encrypted/internal with zero customer release, and the whole paid order awaits administrator review without automatic purchase or compensation. A later explicitly approved internal-wallet compensation policy, if any, must have separate financial idempotency proof.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. Given a timed-out supplier request, when the result is unknown, then the order remains pending reconciliation and the system does not assume failure or refund immediately.
2. Given a confirmed supplier fulfillment, when the response is replayed, then delivery is recorded only once.
3. Given a supplier request for five units that returns three verified items, retain those three encrypted as internally acquired, release zero items to the customer and keep the full order paid/unfulfilled under administrator review; repeated status/export calls and restarts must not buy extra items, release a partial order or automatically compensate it.
4. Given a purchase submission times out after an unknown remote side effect, when a status lookup later identifies the supplier result, then the original request and its submit/lookup/export attempts remain linked and ordered; no second purchase occurs without independently proven provider safety.

### User Story 6 — Migrate historical data safely, one brand at a time (P1)

The owner can transition brands individually to the new platform while retaining financial history and customer obligations. Brand A's legacy catalog is used as the new master catalog; active stock begins empty by deliberate operator action.

**Independent Test**: Restore protected source copies into a rehearsal, import each brand twice, compare identities, balances, paid/unfulfilled obligations and historical orders, then conduct a staged cutover and rollback drill.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. Given the same Telegram ID in two old databases, when migrated, then separate brand-scoped customer rows and wallets exist.
2. Given identical historical order IDs in different sources, when migrated, then source provenance prevents collision or history loss.
3. Given active stock has been deliberately emptied, when catalog migration runs, then no legacy credential stock is imported as available; prior sold credentials and historical order snapshots remain retained.
4. Given one brand cut over while another still operates on legacy code, when customers purchase stock, then the two systems cannot independently allocate the same unit.
5. Given a brand scheduled for migration, when cutover begins, then its selling and inbound payment/update writers are stopped, pending financial events and deliveries are reconciled, and a verified snapshot is captured before its data is imported.

### User Story 7 — Independently operated AI support (P2)

AI support can be maintained, disabled and deployed independently without impacting shop purchases, payment handling or human support.

**Independent Test**: Stop the AI service while processing a purchase and a human support request; both succeed without any AI dependency.

### User Story 8 — Expire unpaid external-payment orders after 30 minutes (P1)

An Iranian or international customer entering bank/crypto payment sees a prominent **30-minute payment validity** warning and the exact deadline. If no qualifying payment evidence arrives before that deadline, the order becomes expired and its existing Telegram payment message is edited to show **Failed — payment window expired**. Confirmed paid orders and timely receipts waiting on review/confirmation are never expired by this rule.

**Independent Test**: Freeze time around a 30-minute deadline, race expiry against payment notification and receipt submission, restart the worker, and verify exactly one correct order transition, a durable Telegram edit attempt and reconciliation of any real late transfer.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. Given an unpaid crypto/bank order at minute 30 with no timely payment evidence or submitted receipt, when the expiry worker runs, then it changes `payment_status` to `expired` and edits the original payment message to failed/expired (or records a retry if Telegram is unavailable).
2. Given a bank receipt submitted at minute 25, when an administrator reviews it hours later, then the order is still reviewable and any confirmed paid/unfulfilled obligation may remain pending restock indefinitely.
3. Given qualifying crypto-payment evidence observed before the deadline and final confirmations after it, when confirmations settle, then the payment remains eligible for reconciliation rather than being marked expired solely because confirmation was delayed.
4. Given a real payment arrives after the expiry boundary, when the provider webhook is processed, then the transfer is recorded once and goes to a defined compensation/reconciliation path; an expired order is not silently reactivated.

### User Story 9 — Paid local-preorder FIFO (P1)

A customer pays for a local preorder and receives the complete requested quantity together once enough stock exists; insufficient stock never causes partial allocation or customer release. New inventory automatically serves earlier paid orders before later ones, including across brands sharing the same product. Customer messages expose only their own order and queue position.

**Independent Test**: Use the A-five/B-two sequence in SC-034 with concurrent payment confirmation, inventory additions and worker restart. Prove oldest-paid complete-order allocation, no smaller-order bypass, no duplicate allocation/payment and recoverable delivery of one complete logical order.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. Given older paid order A for five units and later paid B for two, three stock units complete neither order and are not partially assigned; after three more arrive, A atomically receives all five and B waits with only one unit available.
2. Given a queued paid order and replayed restock/drain work, recovery preserves payment/assignments and never releases a subset or creates another logical order delivery.
3. Given a supplier shortfall, local restocking does not silently resolve or re-purchase that remainder; administrator review remains required.

**Policy gates**: Remaining admin cancellation state/hold transition design (requests use support under FR-070), full-set delivery guards and historical staging partial-order reconciliation remain under review. The new target does not use per-unit partial coupon redemption. No external refund API or recording is introduced.

### Edge Cases

- User has interacted with two bots but not the third; a global campaign includes the user only in the two eligible delivery audiences.
- Telegram ID is duplicated across source databases but usernames, language and ban status differ; preserve each brand-specific value.
- Empty vs absent product override: an explicit blank optional field must not unexpectedly inherit the base value.
- Stock is depleted after a crypto payment is initiated but before it is verified; compensate on confirmed payment according to FR-019.
- Confirmed bank receipt has no stock; preserve paid/unfulfilled obligation with admin follow-up.
- Payment deadline passes with an unpaid crypto/bank order; persist expiry and edit its original Telegram payment message. A late real transfer may not be dropped or interpreted as an authorized fulfillment.
- Supplier times out after actually fulfilling; retries must not deliver twice.
- Wallet funding, refund and order callback arrive repeatedly or out of order.
- Telegram delivery fails after a committed purchase; retry is durable without another charge.
- Brand-scoped asset, conversation state or document is requested through another brand's context; deny access.
- Current old and new services are active during cutover; only one may own each update, payment callback and stock authority.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

#### Brand and customer isolation

- **FR-001**: The platform MUST support at least three independently branded Telegram storefronts using a single private administrative backend API. A graphical administrator UI is outside this feature's delivery scope.
- **FR-002**: All customer-accessible records and actions MUST be scoped to an identified brand; customer-facing workflows MUST NOT reveal other brands' wallets, orders, identities, assets, support contacts, campaign metadata or shared ownership.
- **FR-003**: The primary customer identity MUST be the pair of brand and Telegram user ID. A separate global customer profile is not required. The physical `users.id` is an internal surrogate **brand-membership ID** with unique `(brand_id,telegram_id)`; customer-owned orders MAY use only this `user_id` if brand consistency with products, coupons and support is enforced **in the database**. Administrative cross-brand lookup by Telegram ID MAY be supported, but never exposed to customers.
- **FR-004**: Each brand MUST have independent bot configuration, language defaults, branding, support policy, payment policy and zero or more required Telegram channels, whose eligibility can be checked before relevant actions.
- **FR-005**: Bot secrets MUST be protected from ordinary administrative pages, reports, logs, exports and customer responses. Administrators MUST authenticate independently from Telegram customer accounts, and privileged changes MUST be audited.
- **FR-006**: All authorized administrators MUST be able to manage all three brands through the unified authenticated administrative backend API; no graphical panel or per-brand staff permission boundary is requested for the first release.

#### Catalog and inventory

- **FR-007**: The new master catalog MUST be initialized from the designated first production store. Other legacy catalogs MUST NOT be silently merged as active products.
- **FR-008**: Each brand MUST be able to choose product visibility, price, currency, compatible payment and purchasing rules independently while sharing a central product definition.
- **FR-009**: Product presentation MUST support field-level inheritance, field-specific overrides and intentional empty optional fields. Editing a base field MUST update inheriting listings without overwriting other overridden fields.
- **FR-010**: Local stock MUST belong to a shared inventory pool, with exactly one successful allocation per available item. No stock is held merely because a checkout or receipt-review flow has begun.
- **FR-011**: Purchases MUST retain an immutable snapshot of the brand-facing description/identity relevant to the transaction, the agreed price and currency, discounts, exchange rate when applicable, and fulfillment terms.

#### Money, checkout and fulfillment

- **FR-012**: Every brand membership MAY have **at most one USD-denominated customer wallet**, identified by `user_id`; wallet and ledger tables MUST NOT repeat a constant currency column or support a separate toman wallet in v1. No automatic cross-brand transfer, balance combination or shared customer wallet is allowed. Toman bank/card settlement remains a payment method, not a wallet.
- **FR-013**: Every wallet movement MUST have a durable, auditable ledger entry and MUST be safe against repeated commands, callbacks and worker restarts.
- **FR-014**: An immediate (non-preorder) wallet-funded local-stock checkout MUST atomically validate funds and stock, allocate available inventory, create the order and debit only the corresponding brand wallet. A losing concurrent buyer MUST receive a rejection with no debit or allocation.
- **FR-015**: Customer-facing delivery MUST be recoverable independently of money movement, with duplicate-suppression across retry and restart.
- **FR-016**: Manual bank receipt submission MUST NOT hold stock. When staff confirms a receipt, fulfillment may occur only after allocation succeeds.
- **FR-017**: An accepted manual payment for which stock is unavailable MUST enter a visible paid/unfulfilled pending-restock state that has no automatic expiration. An administrator MAY explicitly deny an unaccepted receipt or resolve an already-confirmed payment entirely outside AccAbad by manual repayment or an alternative arrangement. **No external refund transfer, proof, amount, reference or confirmation is recorded by the application, and there is no refund API/table.** The bot MUST NOT pay, refund or infer off-platform events. If staff separately change an order state inside AccAbad, audit that state change **without representing it as proof of external payment**. A confirmed paid obligation MUST NOT be erased merely by marking the order denied.
- **FR-018**: A confirmed crypto payment MUST be recorded exactly once even if stock has become unavailable since payment initiation.
- **FR-019**: For an immediate (non-preorder) order, if a verified crypto payment cannot allocate local stock, the **frozen quoted amount the customer was required to pay, denominated in USD accounting units**, MUST be credited exactly once to that brand's **USD wallet**, regardless of network fees or provider settlement differences. USDT-priced checkout values map **numerically 1:1 to USD wallet credits by the operator's explicit internal accounting rule**, not a claim about a real-time token/fiat exchange rate. The immutable order inputs and `payments.amount_due` MUST make this amount reproducible. **The order's held coupon use MUST be released, not redeemed, because no product was delivered; the coupon use becomes available again subject to its other validity rules.** The wallet credit, discount-use release, order/fulfillment compensation state and durable notification intent MUST commit exactly once in the same local transaction after verified payment. The system MUST clearly disclose wallet credit as distinct from an external blockchain refund; no live exchange rate may recalculate the completed quote. Source legacy `USD` wallet balances retain their USD denomination during migration.
- **FR-020**: Supplier-fulfilled orders MUST separately track payment settlement, external request outcome and final fulfillment. Unknown supplier outcomes MUST be reconciled before compensation or a potentially duplicating retry.
- **FR-021**: Every brand-specific order MUST use a globally unique order reference and store a separate authoritative creation timestamp. Public identifiers MUST NOT be relied on as authorization credentials.
- **FR-022**: Payments, wallet credits, allocations and fulfillment status changes MUST be observable and actionable by administrators when incomplete or uncertain.

#### Broadcasts and operations

- **FR-023**: A central campaign MUST support a selected audience in one, multiple or all brands and prepare an independent recipient/delivery record for each eligible membership. A user active in all three may receive three distinctly branded messages.
- **FR-024**: Campaign sends MUST honor per-bot reachability, block state, subscription preferences and delivery throttling; campaigns MUST resume after crashes without silently dropping or duplicating recorded recipients.
- **FR-025**: Human support and commerce MUST remain operational when the independent AI service is stopped, unavailable or disabled. AI access to business data MUST be scoped and must not directly perform financial or fulfillment mutations.
- **FR-026**: Background obligations, especially payment reconciliation, broadcasts, **internal USD-wallet credits** and credential deliveries, MUST survive process restarts and expose retry/audit state. External refunds are handled and recorded solely by administrators outside AccAbad.

#### Migration and release safety

- **FR-027**: Historical customer identities, brand wallets, financial ledgers, orders, payment attempts, deposits, delivery history and original encrypted records needed for outstanding obligations MUST be preserved or explicitly reconciled, regardless of which catalog is selected for the new store.
- **FR-028**: Every migrated record that may collide across legacy databases MUST have stable source provenance and a repeatable migration mapping; rerunning an import MUST NOT duplicate balances or obligations.
- **FR-029**: Starting active inventory empty MUST be a deliberate, audited cutover decision; it MUST NOT silently delete historical fulfillment evidence or unresolved paid orders.
- **FR-030**: Brands MUST cut over one at a time only after protected backup/restore rehearsal and reconciliation. Before migrating a brand, the operator MUST stop sales and all writers for that brand (bot updates, payment callbacks, administrators/website writes, background jobs and delivery consumers), reconcile in-flight payments and obligations, deliberately empty or mark its active source stock unavailable, and capture a verified frozen snapshot. Each update stream, payment callback and stock pool MUST have exactly one authoritative writer during coexistence. Other legacy brands MUST NOT sell from any stock item already available to the new shared inventory.
- **FR-031**: Rollback MUST be tested before release. Any non-backward-compatible data change MUST have an explicit restoration and external-payment reconciliation plan, not simply a binary rollback.
- **FR-032**: Each brand MUST support independent human-support tickets, customer/staff replies, pending/assigned/closed states, and durable staff/customer notifications even when the optional AI service is unavailable. All ticket details, media and staff actions MUST remain brand-scoped and auditable.
- **FR-033**: Confirmed supplier failure MUST retain the payment and unfulfilled liability in an auditable `requires_administrator_resolution` state until a documented payment/provider-specific compensation policy is approved. Timeout/unknown outcomes MUST NOT trigger an automatic refund or wallet credit.
- **FR-034**: Before implementation of new catalog pricing, the operator MUST approve a retained-versus-removed matrix for existing discounts, automatic exchange-rate conversion, stale-rate checks, local-currency rounding, wallet topup eligibility and regional payment restrictions; every retained rule MUST have testable examples and versioned order snapshots.
- **FR-035**: Historical wallet migration MUST establish an authoritative **USD opening balance per `(brand,telegram_id)` membership** at a frozen source checkpoint, distinguish non-posting imported history from new live USD ledger movements, and prove that importing history cannot credit or debit a balance twice. Any actual non-USD wallet balance discovered in a legacy source MUST be explicitly reconciled before cutover and MUST NOT be silently converted into the new USD wallet.
- **FR-036**: Customer-visible campaign execution MUST materialize eligible `(campaign,brand,user_id)` recipients, enforce per-bot throttles and subscription/reachability checks, and retry/reconcile without duplicate recorded sends after restart; `users.id` identifies a single brand membership and resolves the correct Telegram ID.
- **FR-037**: Every new order MUST contain exactly one product type and a positive integer quantity. Its immutable brand-facing product snapshot, per-unit price, currency, discount and exchange-rate facts live in `orders`; each independently fulfilled unit MUST remain identifiable. Removing the proposed `order_items` table is conditional on verifying and preserving any multi-product legacy orders.
- **FR-038**: Discounts MUST be brand-scoped, automatically chosen for the customer using the existing best-applicable-discount flow, and applied consistently to both USDT and toman quotes; the customer MUST NOT manually opt out of a mandatory applicable discount. Retain prepaid product-unit entitlements (100% codes with bounded remaining uses), goodwill/replacement codes, and 10/50/100% campaigns. **Do not create a failed order for invalid quantity**: if the best applicable mandatory 100% discount has 10 remaining uses and a customer enters 11, reject **that quantity entry**, explain the maximum is 10 and invite correction; never silently buy 10 or charge for the 11th item. Revalidate inside the final atomic transaction to handle competing redemptions; invalid quantity consumes no code, stock or wallet balance. Zero-price purchases require no wallet debit. Prepaid obligations keep their paid-source provenance and do not replace FR-019 crypto compensation.
- **FR-039**: Preserve the current Nobitex USDT/rial → toman conversion policy, including its hourly refresh, toman conversion, upward 5,000-toman rounding, stale-rate failure behavior, and per-product `auto_price` opt-out with a manually maintained toman price. **No historical `exchange_rates` table is required**: current rate/freshness fields MAY live on `brands`; every accepted converted toman order MUST snapshot the applied rate and source timestamp. A product with a fixed manual toman price MAY also be bought using its **USD wallet** or a USDT crypto payment based directly on its **independent positive USDT price**; do not prohibit that combination or derive the charge from the manually fixed toman amount.
- **FR-040**: Every new order MUST store `user_id`, `unit_price_usdt`, `currency`, `payment_method`, nullable `unit_price_toman`, `exchange_rate_toman` and related FX source/fetch timestamps (toman/FX may be null for USDT-only payments), quantity, automatic-discount facts, and immutable rounding rule/version facts. **Neither `total_usdt` nor `total_toman` belongs in `orders`**; application read models compute both using these frozen inputs and the retained calculation version. Remove `pricing_mode` and `payable_usd`. Exact requested/received money amounts remain in typed immutable payment attempts (`payments.amount_due`, `payments.amount_received`, `currency`) and wallet ledger entries; computing a total later MUST NOT change any historical settled value.
- **FR-041**: Newly created **unpaid bank/crypto payment** orders MUST have `payment_due_at` exactly 30 minutes after creation and clearly warn of this duration and UTC-derived local deadline wherever payment instructions are displayed. On timeout without qualifying evidence, an idempotent job MUST set `payment_status=expired` (customer-facing **Failed — payment window expired**) and attempt to edit the original Telegram payment message, with durable retries/fallback. Wallet and fully discounted instant purchases do not need a pending-payment expiry.
- **FR-042**: Receipt submission/verified crypto transaction evidence recorded **within** the payment validity window MUST suspend simple unpaid timeout while staff review or chain confirmation proceeds, possibly beyond 30 minutes. A real transfer received after expiry MUST be recorded once and reconciled without silent forfeiture, duplicate credit or automatic order reactivation; the precise late-payment compensation policy is a pre-implementation gate. Paid/unfulfilled pending-restock orders MUST NOT be subject to this unpaid-payment expiry.
- **FR-043**: To keep `orders` compact, `orders.user_id` MAY replace both `orders.brand_id` and `orders.telegram_id`, while `users` has a surrogate PK and unique `(brand_id,telegram_id)`. Removing the redundant brand column requires a database-enforced brand-match check across every order, listing and coupon relation plus cross-brand access tests; deriving brand only in application queries is insufficient.
- **FR-044**: Customer Telegram interactions, checkout, support messages, order updates and campaign deliveries MUST use **private bot-to-customer DMs only** in v1. The recipient Telegram chat ID MUST be resolved through `users.telegram_id`; `telegram_deliveries` MUST NOT duplicate it as `chat_id`. Required Telegram group/channel IDs used solely for forced-join checks remain independently recorded in `brand_channels`. One revision-guarded `users.flow_*` private conversation context per brand membership is sufficient; no `telegram_sessions` table is required.
- **FR-045**: An order's `payment_method` and `currency` MUST be **immutable from creation**. A customer wishing to use another payment method MUST initiate a **new order**. Once the replacement order is successfully created, an older order in `awaiting_payment` for that **same brand membership** MUST be atomically marked `cancelled` (reason: replaced), with its held discount capacity released and original Telegram payment message edited to explain cancellation. If replacement creation fails, the original order and its deadline remain unchanged. Orders already `under_review`, `confirmation_pending`, paid or otherwise carrying qualifying money evidence MUST NOT be automatically cancelled. Payment evidence that later arrives for a cancelled/expired earlier order MUST be recorded and reconciled against that original order rather than transferred to the replacement.
- **FR-046**: Customer-initiated **wallet top-ups MUST use crypto through NOWPayments only**; card-to-card MUST NOT fund customer wallets. Product orders MAY use card-to-card (toman), NOWPayments crypto (USD-valued quote based on the product's USDT price), or an existing USD wallet balance where the respective brand/listing permits it. An external USD-valued checkout MUST NOT silently turn into a card-to-card payment; changing methods requires a new order under FR-045. Wallet amounts shown to customers are labeled **USD**; the platform MUST preserve distinct records for a quoted USD value, the cryptocurrency actually transferred and the payment-network amount. A stored USD wallet balance represents brand-specific **store credit**, not a promise of an individually held on-chain stablecoin balance.
- **FR-047**: Standalone NOWPayments wallet-funding attempts MUST be represented without fictitious product orders. A payment MUST refer to **exactly one** typed origin: `order_id` for external order settlement or `topup_user_id` for customer-initiated wallet funding. A wallet-funding payment belongs to the correct brand membership, uses a frozen USD funding quote, and credits the brand's USD ledger/wallet only after verified and deduplicated settlement. Previously agreed wallet-topup eligibility restrictions, including zero-balance policy, remain in force until separately changed. **The owner-approved crypto pay-asset allowlist is exactly USDT on TRON/TRC20 (`USDTTRC20`) and USDT on BNB Smart Chain/BEP20 (`USDTBSC`)**; native TRX, BNB, other tokens and other USDT networks MUST NOT be offered or silently substituted. Provider availability/codes are verified before invoice creation.
- **FR-048**: All three brands MUST use **one shared NOWPayments merchant account** with one centrally managed credential set and verified callback integration; the platform MUST NOT require three merchant accounts, replicate the shared provider credentials into each `brands` row or add a merchant-account table in v1. Every provider payment reference MUST bind to exactly one stored `payments` row, whose `order_id` or `topup_user_id` determines its originating brand. Invoice creation MUST use an internally generated unique payment reference and brand-specific customer-facing description without cross-brand names or logos; callbacks MUST resolve brand from the persisted payment, not an untrusted provider `order_description`, caller-supplied brand ID or Telegram ID. Shared gateway operation MUST NOT merge wallets, payment accounting, refunds or admin/audit attribution between brands. A pre-launch privacy check MUST inspect actual hosted checkout, merchant/payee metadata, receipts and supported invoice labeling, because a shared account may reveal the common merchant identity despite independently branded Telegram bots.
- **FR-049**: Each Iranian bank/card-enabled brand MUST use its **own distinct receiving bank card**, not the other brand's card; the international brand MAY have no bank destination. The owner CONFIRMS **exactly one active receiving card per bank-enabled brand**: each successfully committed administrator replacement immediately makes the **newest saved card** the sole active destination for **new** orders. Keep its protected, typed details on `brands`, not a new bank-card table or list. Apply version-checked atomic replacement so conflicting concurrent admin changes cannot silently overwrite each other. Each bank payment MUST preserve an immutable, protected snapshot of the card and recipient identity shown at order creation, so later card replacement cannot rewrite historical payment instructions or receipt-dispute evidence. Card details MUST NOT leak into another brand's customer messages, logs or exports. A changed receiving card applies only to newly created orders; a pending old order retains its original 30-minute instructions and any timely receipt is still reviewed against its original destination. Separate card numbers do **not** guarantee separate displayed bank-account-holder identities; operators MUST verify actual bank transfer preview disclosure.
- **FR-050**: Automatic discounts on bank/crypto orders MUST acquire a transactionally checked, **temporary claim on eligible coupon uses**, not inventory stock, when the order enters `awaiting_payment`. The 30-minute unpaid expiry or replacement cancellation MUST release that claim; a timely submitted bank receipt (`under_review`) or qualifying on-time crypto evidence (`confirmation_pending`) MUST preserve it beyond 30 minutes while the payment decision remains unresolved. Accepted paid bank orders awaiting restock retain the claim until successful fulfillment or an explicit audited financial resolution. Fully discounted (100%-free) and wallet-funded **immediate local-stock** checkout MUST validate, allocate and redeem coupon uses atomically with no external-payment hold; asynchronous supplier orders use FR-073's whole held-claim lifecycle. `discount_uses` MUST represent `held/redeemed/released` lifecycle without adding a new physical table. The source's `discounts.redeemed_count` counts only committed redemptions, while held usage also counts against remaining allocatable capacity; locks or equivalent serialization MUST prevent exceeding either cap. A customer cannot use pending orders to bypass quantity limits or temporarily monopolize a capped campaign: at most one `awaiting_payment` order per brand membership, with anti-abuse controls for payment-evidence review flows.
- **FR-051**: A brand membership MUST have **at most one order with payment status `awaiting_payment`** at a time. Creating a valid new order MUST atomically cancel only the prior `awaiting_payment` order, release its temporary discount claim and create the replacement with its own immutable quote, payment method and deadline. A unique partial database index on `orders(user_id)` in `awaiting_payment` state MUST enforce this across concurrently delivered Telegram updates; user-level ordering/locking and idempotency MUST prevent races. An unconfirmed real bank/crypto transfer may already be on the way despite cancellation; provider attempts/evidence MUST remain independently traceable and late funds must be reconciled, not discarded or automatically assigned to the new order. A replacement cancellation is visibly **Cancelled — replaced by a new order**, distinct from 30-minute **Failed/Expired**. `under_review`, `confirmation_pending`, and paid/restock states do not count as replaceable unpaid orders, and must remain protected.
- **FR-052**: **Owner-approved option A for paid crypto without stock**: when immediate (non-preorder) all-or-nothing local allocation fails after verified NOWPayments payment, the user MUST both receive the exact frozen eligible **USD wallet compensation** under FR-019 **and retain every automatically selected coupon use** from that undelivered order. Transition the existing `discount_uses` hold to `released` exactly once; it MUST NOT increment `discounts.redeemed_count`. The final order must show payment received but no goods delivered and compensation fulfilled, not a fictitious successful fulfillment. Replayed callbacks, duplicated jobs and concurrent stock attempts MUST NOT double-credit the wallet or release another order's coupon. A released campaign coupon remains subject to its original configured expiration/eligibility; returning a use does not lengthen its expiration (see FR-053).
- **FR-053**: **Owner-approved option A for restored discount expiration**: releasing a held coupon use after payment-window expiry, replacement cancellation or compensated crypto/no-stock failure MUST NOT change `discounts.ends_at`, reactivate an expired promotion or create an automatic replacement coupon. A restored use on an expired marketing campaign remains auditable but cannot be selected for a **new** order. A coupon validly held when an order was created MUST continue to apply to that protected order if timely receipt/provider evidence carries review or confirmation beyond the coupon's own expiry; final fulfillment MUST redeem the original held use without rechecking campaign expiry as a new checkout. **Prepaid 100%-off future-unit entitlements are distinct from time-limited marketing promotions**: they MUST NOT be silently forfeited by automated flows while a paid undelivered liability remains, although an explicit authenticated business-owner override is allowed under FR-058; v1 issuance defaults to `ends_at IS NULL` for prepaid codes, subject to explicit separately approved customer-visible paid-contract terms. - **FR-054**: **Owner-approved option A for prepaid coupon ownership**: newly issued `discounts.kind='prepaid_entitlement'` codes MUST be bound to exactly **one brand membership** through non-null `discounts.eligible_user_id → users.id`; that membership MUST match the paid source order's customer and the coupon brand. Knowledge, possession or forwarding of the code alone MUST NOT permit a different Telegram account or the same Telegram ID in another brand to redeem it. Prepaid claims MUST be validated against the authenticated membership on eligibility, display, hold and redemption, with authoritative database checks preventing cross-brand/source mismatch. New prepaid issuance uses one multi-use code under FR-055; goodwill and public campaigns retain their independently declared eligibility scopes.
- **FR-055**: **Owner-approved option A for prepaid bulk-code issuance**: a new prepaid order purchasing `N` future units of one product MUST issue **one account-bound 100%-off coupon with `max_total_uses=N`**, not `N` separately managed one-use codes. Without an owner override, its remaining allowance is `N - redeemed_count - currently held units`; after owner-set usage adjustments, available allowance is `max(0,max_total_uses - max(0,redeemed_count + usage_adjustment_units) - held units)` when checking availability; each redeemed order records its own `discount_uses.units_covered` equal to units actually fulfilled. The same coupon MAY cover multiple later one-product orders of varying quantities, up to the purchased units, without allocating stock in advance or charging a second time for covered units. An order requesting more units than its available coupon capacity MUST be rejected at quantity selection without a partial redemption or failed order. The paid source order and cumulative entitlement redemption MUST remain auditable. This choice is the **default for new prepaid issuance** and does not delete historical one-use codes or prohibit separate goodwill/marketing code batches.
- **FR-056**: **Owner-approved mandatory quantity behavior for all automatic percentage discounts**: when an otherwise eligible 10%, 50% or 100% coupon cannot cover every requested unit because its remaining total or per-customer usage cap is lower than quantity, checkout MUST reject the **quantity input**, show the maximum eligible quantity and invite correction. It MUST NOT silently split one order into discounted and full-price units, skip a mandatory coupon, create a failed order or reserve stock/funds. Revalidate held plus redeemed capacity atomically at final creation and fulfillment.
- **FR-057**: **Owner-approved late crypto handling**: a USDT payment verified as fully settled against an original immutable NOWPayments quote that arrives **after** the 30-minute deadline or after replacement cancellation MUST NOT reactivate or fulfill the old order. If full quote satisfaction, correct original `payments` binding, correct selected network, confirmed amount and callback authenticity can be proven, credit that **original brand membership's USD store wallet exactly once for the frozen eligible USD quote** and record a durable notification/ledger link. Underpaid, excess, wrong-network, disputed or uncertain receipts MUST require human reconciliation rather than automatic credit. Late manual bank transfers remain staff-reviewed. A prior expired/cancelled coupon hold remains released; no expired promotional use is consumed or extended. The original order retains its historical expired/cancelled reason with an explicit compensated-payment exception recorded through existing typed payment/ledger/audit state; never silently assign money to a replacement order.
- **FR-058**: **Owner-directed off-bot administration and unrestricted coupon business edits**: the bot MUST NOT infer an administrator's external bank refund, manual delivery or off-platform settlement; paid-bank/restock state and coupon holds stay unchanged until explicit staff action. To keep actual redemption evidence honest, preserve `discounts.redeemed_count` as finalized redemption facts and store `discounts.usage_adjustment_units` as a signed typed admin adjustment: effective used = `max(0,redeemed_count + usage_adjustment_units)`; setting an absolute used count updates only the adjustment, never old `discount_uses` rows. New availability = `max(0,max_total_uses - effective_used - currently held units)`; edits that overcommit a coupon prevent **new** holds without silently cancelling earlier valid holds. Every edit must be authenticated, serialized against checkout and recorded in `audit_events` with actor, before/after values, time and reason, but does not require prior approval or proof of refund. New orders use current coupon terms; old order price snapshots, actual redemption evidence, brand identity and source-order provenance remain intact. Owner-set expiration overrides the default non-expiring prepaid issuance policy.

- **FR-064**: **Owner-approved multiple supplier sources with manual selection**: one shared shop product MUST support several supplier mappings, each identifying the provider, external product ID and a separate optional external variant ID. **One configured account per supplier is sufficient for v1**, shared across its product mappings and all three brands; multiple accounts/account pools per provider are out of scope. Resolve the provider's one protected account configuration rather than duplicating credentials or an independently selectable account on each mapping. Paid requests still preserve the actual account identity for historical reconciliation; credential rotation must not redirect them to another account. No automatic cheapest-source selection, fallback or split purchasing is authorized. Source changes MUST be authenticated, revision-checked and audited. A local intent key does not prove remote idempotency. The supplier is fixed at accepted payment under FR-066, not unpaid order creation. Queue by durable accepted-payment time plus order-ID tie-breaker per shared product across brands; unpaid quotes/unaccepted receipts/unverified payments get no paid priority. Allocate the oldest eligible order only when its **entire requested quantity** is available, then assign all units atomically and persist complete-order delivery work. With fewer units, allocate none, reserve no subset and stop the drain; newer/smaller orders cannot bypass that older order. Restocking durably schedules replay-safe drain work serialized with every allocator consuming the product's stock. Paid queued orders do not expire under the unpaid timer or get immediate-sale shortage credit. Supplier shortfalls remain separate administrator-reviewed procurement, not local FIFO. Keep availability policy separate from fulfillment source. Remaining admin cancellation state/hold transition design, full-set transport guards and legacy staging partial-order reconciliation remain review gates. External refunds/records remain off-platform.

- **FR-066**: **Owner-approved supplier binding at accepted payment and unavailable-source default**: for a supplier-fulfilled product order, use the then-current shared master-product source when payment is accepted: successful committed USD-wallet debit, verified crypto payment accepted for the live order, or administrator-approved bank payment. Unpaid order creation, invoice opening and unapproved receipt submission MUST NOT fix the supplier. Commit accepted payment state, immutable source/account/external-product/optional-variant identity and durable procurement intent together under serialized order/source state; no remote supplier purchase occurs inside that database transaction. Preserve the customer's original product/price/currency terms. If the selected source is disabled, missing or known unable to supply, still retain any accepted payment and the paid/unfulfilled obligation, with a typed reason requiring administrator review; retain the selected identity where known and do not invent a usable binding or runnable purchase intent when none exists. Unavailable-source discovery after binding likewise retains payment and the original source. No silent supplier switch, automatic new purchase, wallet compensation, external refund or fictitious fulfillment is authorized. Repeated payment callbacks/workers MUST reuse the original accepted state and snapshot, not current catalog routing; source-switch/payment races must yield one consistent source revision. Later repair or re-enablement MUST NOT automatically bypass the administrator-review state. Existing late/expired-payment recovery rules still apply; late money routed to USD wallet recovery does not create a supplier purchase. For a supplier order fully covered by a valid 100%-off coupon, source binding is at committed no-payment-required checkout under FR-073; no actual payment is fabricated. Physical typed blocker/snapshot and zero-due acceptance fields remain pre-DDL design review items.

- **FR-067**: **Owner-corrected complete-order delivery, no intentional partial fulfillment**: for new target orders, requesting five items means one complete five-item fulfillment, across local, supplier and local-preorder flows. Prepare and verify the complete order manifest before releasing any customer-accessible credential or delivery-content file, link or message. Payment confirmations, waiting-status notices and support messages remain allowed while fulfillment is pending. Internally acquired supplier subsets are durable encrypted evidence, not delivered goods or available shared stock. Preserve per-unit identity/deduplication but guard customer release and order completion at whole-order level. Coupon claims remain held in full while an order is waiting/incomplete; redeem the entire claim exactly once with complete-order allocation/fulfillment, not as individual supplier outputs arrive. Existing independently approved whole-order expiry/compensation release rules remain; approved paid-local-preorder cancellation releases the full still-held claim under FR-070 / ADR-44. Do not infer release from unknown outside actions. Telegram may require multiple messages/files for the complete prepared manifest; an interrupted send resumes that same manifest without new purchase, payment, coupon redemption or intentional partial-order completion. Do not promise atomic remote Telegram delivery. This supersedes earlier target approvals/examples of delivering three now and two later, and removes the proposed partial-unit coupon-choice requirement. Preserve actual historical partial deliveries without erasure, reclassification or duplicate redelivery; migration needs an explicitly reviewed compatibility plan.

- **FR-068**: **Owner-approved first-release supplier product scope**: automated supplier procurement MUST support only ready-to-deliver accounts, keys and codes that the provider can fulfill without collecting customer email, existing-account credentials, OTPs or other customer-supplied fulfillment inputs. Email activation, slot assignment requiring a buyer email, upgrades to an existing customer account and manual/custom supplier delivery are outside this first-release supplier scope. Normal customer use/redemption of an already delivered key/code is not an input-collecting supplier workflow. Validate eligibility for the exact external product/variant at mapping enablement/publication and revalidate before a remote purchase; missing or unverified fulfillment requirements MUST NOT be assumed eligible. If known ineligible before payment, reject new supplier checkout before charging or creating an invoice. If eligibility changes/is discovered after accepted payment, retain the original bound source and paid/unfulfilled obligation for administrator review under FR-066; no fallback purchase, automatic compensation or lost payment. Provider processing/status lookup/export may still be asynchronous; ready-to-deliver describes the product/required inputs, not a promise of synchronous success. Keep complete-order release under FR-067. Do not add customer-input forms, generic input payloads or new activation/upgrade tables for this excluded scope; separately approved local/manual product behavior is unchanged.

Do not expose/store an active preorder date/ETA field or quoted-date snapshot, require/synthesize a date, or create date-change notification/expiry jobs. A confirmed-paid preorder remains queued indefinitely until its entire quantity is allocatable or staff separately perform an explicit authorized resolution; elapsed waiting time alone never cancels/expires/reorders it, releases coupons or creates credit/refund. Oldest-paid full-order FIFO, no smaller-order bypass and the existing support channel remain unchanged. Technical created/paid timestamps and the previously approved 30-minute **unpaid external-payment** window remain separate; that payment window is not a time cap on paid preorder fulfillment. Preserve/reconcile actual legacy date promises as historical source evidence under FR-027, without making them new target product settings or silently deleting history. This supersedes the earlier optional-date interpretation and eliminates its pending notification question.

- **FR-070**: **Owner-approved paid-preorder cancellation requests through support**: customers requesting cancellation of a paid local preorder waiting for stock MUST use the existing brand-scoped support-ticket conversation. Reuse `support_tickets.order_id` where the customer/brand/order match; another membership's order reference must be rejected. No dedicated cancellation-request button, endpoint, table or separate cancellation workflow is introduced. Staff review and respond manually through existing support/admin facilities. Creating, assigning, replying to, closing or reopening a ticket MUST NOT itself pause/reorder FIFO, cancel the order, release coupon uses, move money, issue another supplier purchase or imply an external refund. The paid order remains under its existing rules unless staff separately perform an authorized, audited order-state action; free-text messages/support status are not order or financial authority. Serialize any actual order resolution against stock allocation so stale workers cannot allocate an already resolved order. Preserve original payment/obligation history; no external payout details or proof are required/stored. **Owner-approved cancellation payment policy:** after an administrator approves cancellation of a paid local preorder, the administrator handles repayment outside the shop. Cancellation MUST NOT create a wallet credit, reverse an original wallet debit, execute a payout or store outside payout amounts/references/receipts/status. Preserve the original accepted payment, debit and obligation history; an audited cancellation records the order-state action only and MUST NOT claim or prove that repayment occurred. Do not infer outside repayment from ticket text/status. Approving cancellation of a paid local preorder MUST atomically release **all** of that order's still-held coupon uses (`held → released`) exactly once with the authorized cancellation and actor/reason audit. No separate coupon action or proof of outside repayment is required. Preserve the claim/history and original coupon expiry, activation, eligibility, limits, actual redeemed count and usage-adjustment values; do not create a replacement coupon, renew an expired campaign or increase its cap. Returned capacity is usable only under the coupon's current ordinary eligibility and limits. Serialize cancellation with complete-order allocation/fulfillment: only one outcome commits; a stale/replayed cancellation cannot release already redeemed uses or permit post-cancellation allocation. Support activity alone still releases nothing. Repayment remains outside the shop with no cancellation wallet credit or payout records. Exact admin state/hold representation remains a pre-DDL design review; independently approved late-crypto and immediate-sale shortage compensation stay unchanged.

- **FR-071**: **Owner-approved 180-day nonfinancial bot activity retention**: retain operational `bot_activity_events` for a rolling **180 elapsed days** from their original trusted `occurred_at`, using UTC server time. Cleanup permissions, batch/index design, scheduling/monitoring and backup/restore handling require implementation review; no live collection/deletion is authorized by this specification choice.

- **FR-072**: **Owner-approved supplier bonuses go to administrators if actually supplied**: If a supplier actually provides verified bonus accounts, keys or codes beyond the customer's ordered quantity, route those extras to authorized administrators through the existing admin facilities with protected content access. The customer receives the complete ordered quantity only; bonuses are excluded from every customer manifest, download, history and delivery path. Retain bonus content encrypted and attributable to its original supplier request/account/product and item identity; deduplicate purchase/export replays so the same item cannot become both a paid customer unit and an admin bonus. Do not increase order quantity, customer fulfillment rows, coupon usage or payment, automatically add bonuses to saleable shared inventory, or buy extra units to obtain a bonus. Do not expose credentials in logs, activity events, ordinary audit text or unprotected notices. Ambiguous extras or unprovable item identity require admin review rather than invented bonus attribution. - **FR-073**: **Owner-approved automatic supplier procurement for valid 100%-off checkout**: When a valid automatically selected 100%-off coupon covers every requested unit of an eligible supplier product, checkout automatically accepts the zero-due order and schedules purchase from the selected supplier without a separate administrator approval. Revalidate membership/brand/product eligibility, whole-quantity coupon capacity and the selected eligible source in one serialized local checkout transaction; commit the full coupon claim as `held`, `no_payment_required` acceptance, immutable source/account/product/variant revision and durable supplier procurement work together. This committed acceptance is the source-binding point for zero-due supplier orders. No customer wallet balance is required, no wallet debit/credit or fabricated external payment/invoice is created, and no unpaid-payment timer applies. Call the supplier only after commit using the original request/intent and existing provider-specific uncertainty rules. Hold the whole coupon claim while procurement is pending/unknown/incomplete; redeem it exactly once with complete-order fulfillment, not merely on checkout or supplier submission. Invalid coupon/quantity/source before commit leaves no accepted order, hold or procurement work. Existing full-order delivery, verified-admin-only bonus routing, owner coupon-edit rights and local/immediate-sale rules remain unchanged.

- **FR-074**: **Owner-approved 10% supplier cost-increase allowance**: For supplier procurement after order acceptance, allow a supplier cost increase of **up to and including 10%** automatically; a greater increase requires administrator review before purchasing. This is supplier cost, not the customer's selling price or discounted amount; the threshold also applies to 100%-off orders. Keep the customer quote/payment unchanged and absorb permitted increases. Use exact decimal arithmetic: `current_cost * 100 <= baseline_cost * 110`; unchanged/lower verified costs pass this increase guard. Never reset the baseline on retry, source edits or sequential price changes. Missing/nonpositive/unverifiable baseline, currency/quantity mismatch or unverified payable cost requires review rather than a guessed percentage. Preserve accepted payment/no-payment-required obligation, held coupons and original source during review, with no fallback purchase, automatic credit/refund or price-driven coupon release. Later price reduction alone does not clear a persisted review blocker; staff handle the case through an authenticated audited action tied to that request and explicit approved cost ceiling. Existing whole-order and unknown-purchase safeguards remain. A catalog read followed by an uncapped purchase is insufficient proof. Without that capability/evidence, route to admin review; do not claim that this policy proves current adapters enforce a cost cap. Provider calls stay outside the acceptance transaction. If a purchase may already have happened, reconcile the original attempt rather than retrying or treating a later price check as proof of no charge.

- **FR-075**: **Owner-corrected visible out-of-stock supplier listings**: Keep an otherwise active, brand-published supplier product visible when its selected source cannot supply it, labeled **Out of stock**, with new purchases blocked. Recheck current source/eligibility/quantity availability before accepting a new wallet, external-payment or fully discounted checkout; stale/forged buy callbacks cannot bypass the block. No new wallet debit, coupon claim, accepted zero-due order or payment invoice is created for known unavailable checkout. Catalog status does not cancel/reprice existing accepted orders, release their coupon claims or clear paid-order admin review; accepted-payment races retain obligations under FR-066/073. ### Key Entities

**Database design constraint (owner preference, 2026-10-28)**: The new backend schema SHALL have zero PostgreSQL `json`/`jsonb` columns and no generic EAV settings or serialized worker-state fields. Brand/payment policies, product overrides, quote/brand presentation snapshots, campaign audiences and worker origins must have explicit relational types/constraints. Multi-valued data may require justified FK/junction tables. The owner-approved **27-table** candidate in [database-table-review.md](database-table-review.md) includes the **confirmed private-DM and USD-wallet-only simplifications**, but remains conditional on legacy order compatibility; it does not authorize losing historical obligations.

**Encryption scope**: Brand-owned sensitive data and bot tokens use new separate per-brand keyrings. Shared unsold inventory credentials require their **own independently restricted platform-inventory keyring**, because they have no customer brand until sale. No bot consumer receives blanket permission to decrypt the shared stock pool. All source-specific legacy keys stay available for verified migration and protected recovery until their backups expire.

- **Bot Activity Event**: Append-only, per-brand-membership customer interaction and flow outcome evidence with typed references and replay dedupe; never raw Telegram input.
- **Brand**: Independent storefront identity and business policy; associated with a Telegram bot, protected credentials, required-channel rules, media and support configuration.
- **User**: Brand-scoped Telegram customer, uniquely identified by `(brand, telegram_id)` and referenced by one internal `users.id`; independent language, membership, ban and preference state.
- **Admin User**: Authorized operator with global brand access and audit trail.
- **Product / Brand Product / Product Supplier Source**: Internal master catalog, inheriting/overridden brand listing, and approved table 27 linking each master product to provider product/optional variant mappings; one active selection is shared across brands.
- **Inventory Item / Allocation**: Shared stock and its single authoritative assignment to a fulfilled or fulfilling order.
- **Wallet / Wallet Transaction**: One optional **USD wallet** per brand membership and immutable USD-denominated financial movements; the purchase quote itself can be in USDT or toman.
- **Order / Unit Fulfillment**: Purchase of one product type with quantity, membership owner, immutable per-unit/discount/FX/rounding inputs, a 30-minute due timestamp when awaiting external payment and separate per-unit delivery obligations; totals are calculated, not stored on orders.
- **Discount / Discount Use / Discount Revision**: Product/brand-scoped prepaid entitlement with bounded unit uses, goodwill code or campaign discount, automatically applied across both quote currencies with strict all-requested-unit coverage.
- **Current FX State**: Type-safe per-brand fields for the latest Nobitex rate/freshness, with accepted conversion snapshots on orders and a per-product `auto_price_toman` switch. No separate history table.
- **Payment Attempt / Manual Deposit / Compensation**: External money evidence, staff review and exactly-once financial remedy.
- **Fulfillment / Supplier Request / Supplier Request Attempt / Job / Telegram Delivery**: Individually traceable encrypted delivered units, one order-level supplier batch, immutable remote submit/lookup/export evidence, leased work and recoverable outgoing messaging as separate concerns.
- **Campaign / Telegram Delivery**: Per-brand/language campaign under an optional shared launch batch, with one materialized delivery row per eligible bot membership.
- **Legacy Identity Map**: Source-instance, source-table and source-key to new canonical record mapping.

## Success Criteria

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: In the cross-brand isolation acceptance suite, **zero** foreign-brand customer records, media, support identities, wallet amounts or historical orders are exposed.
- **SC-002**: In 50 simultaneous wallet purchase attempts for one local stock item, **exactly one** succeeds, one unit is allocated, and every rejected buyer's balance remains unchanged.
- **SC-003**: Replaying the same paid provider event 100 times results in **exactly one** financial settlement and, where applicable, **one** wallet credit.
- **SC-004**: Repeated interruption and restart tests result in **zero** lost recorded delivery/payment obligations and **zero** duplicated debits or stock allocations.
- **SC-005**: Every production cutover brand achieves **100% reconciliation** of recorded opening balances and outstanding paid obligations against its approved source snapshot, with every discrepancy resolved or explicitly signed off before release.
- **SC-006**: An authenticated administrator can configure a product for three brands, inspect all brand orders and launch a scoped campaign through one backend API without switching store instances.
- **SC-007**: Existing purchase, staff support and payment confirmation journeys remain available when AI support is unavailable.
- **SC-008**: At least **95%** of preselected user acceptance participants complete a basic catalog-to-checkout journey without staff assistance; benchmark targets for latency and resource use are set only after representative baseline measurements.
- **SC-009**: With AI disabled, a customer can open a human-support ticket and receive a staff reply under the correct brand, with no cross-brand disclosure or lost messages after worker restart.
- **SC-010**: A three-brand campaign produces one recipient record per eligible membership, survives interruption/restart, and applies independent per-bot rate limits without losing recorded recipient obligations.
- **SC-011**: If one eligible 100% discount has 10 remaining product-unit uses, requesting 11 displays an actionable "Maximum 10" quantity correction without creating an order, consuming a discount, allocating stock or debiting a wallet in either currency; a corrected request for 10 succeeds atomically if stock exists.
- **SC-012**: Automatic toman quotes reproduce the legacy Nobitex/5,000-toman ceiling rule and fail closed on stale rates; manually priced listings remain unaffected by rate refresh and can still accept independent USDT pricing. USDT-only orders allow nullable toman/rate fields; historical computed totals reproduce the original amount charged on the relevant payment/ledger.
- **SC-013**: Every unpaid external-payment order displays a 30-minute deadline; timeout changes order state once and queues a Telegram edit. Timely receipts, qualifying pending confirmations and fully paid unfulfilled orders are never incorrectly expired, and late money is always retained for reconciliation.
- **SC-014**: Brand-scoped `user_id` lookup routes to the correct bot/Telegram ID, and attempts to attach a user to another brand's listing/discount fail database constraints without leaking cross-brand records.
- **SC-015**: `wallets` has PK `user_id` and no currency column; `wallet_entries` has no currency column, and USD posting/compensation is unchanged. The three brands may each hold a different USD balance for the same Telegram ID.
- **SC-016**: Every outbound customer notification uses its membership's `users.telegram_id` for the private chat destination, and persists no duplicate `telegram_deliveries.chat_id`; channel join checks continue to use the independently stored `brand_channels.chat_id`.
- **SC-017**: Attempting to switch an existing unpaid order's payment method is rejected with an option to create a new order. Successful new-order creation atomically cancels an older `awaiting_payment` order for the same membership and releases its unused discount hold; the original method/currency and audit evidence remain frozen. - Time-limited promotional coupons keep their original expiry on release; prepaid future-unit entitlements default to no expiry because they represent undelivered paid value. Any nonstandard prepaid expiry or off-bot liability discharge requires separate approval and clear customer terms.

- A lightweight C# Native AOT runtime, unified admin interface and independently deployed AI service are architectural goals to verify during `/speckit-plan`; they do not alter the business acceptance requirements above.
- No production database, payment or Telegram write is authorized by this specification.

## Resolved Clarifications and Release Gates

1. **Manual bank receipts**: Paid/unfulfilled may remain pending indefinitely. Staff may reject an unconfirmed receipt or arrange direct off-platform resolution of a confirmed payment. Neither the external refund nor its evidence is recorded by AccAbad; only an explicit, separate staff order-state update, if any, is audited.
2. **Crypto amount and currency**: Credit the brand's **USD** wallet for the frozen dollar-valued amount the customer should have paid under the checkout quote, exactly once. USDT prices are converted numerically 1:1 to USD wallet-credit units by business policy; preserve legacy `USD` wallet labels, do not rename them.
3. **Gradual cutover**: Empty/disable the brand's active inventory, fully stop its old writers, reconcile in-flight financial and fulfillment obligations, snapshot and migrate that brand. Never make the same stock item simultaneously saleable by both old and new platforms.

These decisions settle the feature-spec questions but do **not** constitute proof that a particular live provider, source schema or deployment configuration supports safe cutover.
