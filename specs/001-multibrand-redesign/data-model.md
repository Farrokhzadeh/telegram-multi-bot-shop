# Logical Data Model and States

**Status**: Proposed schema contract; not deployable DDL. IDs are scoped and historical mappings remain explicit. The **28-table no-JSON relational candidate** (the original 23-table base plus owner-approved `discount_revisions`, `bot_activity_events`, `supplier_request_attempts`, `product_supplier_sources` and `product_stock_subscriptions`), complete typed columns and outstanding decisions are in [database-table-review.md](database-table-review.md). The owner confirmed **USD-only wallets and private-DM-only bot conversations**; no `telegram_sessions` table or customer-chat IDs in outgoing deliveries. Historical multi-product order representation may still increase the table count. No historical exchange-rate table.

**Relational constraint**: The target schema has **no `json`/`jsonb` or generic EAV columns**. Orders retain typed immutable quote/presentation fields, provider state has typed evidence pointers, jobs have checked foreign keys, and brand policy uses explicit versioned columns. Schema additions require reviewed migrations.

## Approved source selection and local-preorder semantics (2026-10-29)

**Table 27 and behavior owner-approved; final constraints pending review.** One shop product has many supplier mappings and one administrator-selected active source for new procurement. Approved relation `product_supplier_sources`, with reviewed candidate fields: `id`, `product_id`, `provider_code` (resolves the supplier's one configured account), `external_product_id`, nullable `external_variant_id`, `enabled`, `revision`, timestamps. Use one checked active-source pointer or one filtered unique selection, never two authorities; selection scope is owner-approved at the shared master product, affecting all three brands together with no per-brand override. If a pointer is chosen it belongs to `products`, not `brand_products`; if a filtered selection is chosen, uniqueness is by `product_id`, not `(brand_id,product_id)`. One configured account per provider is owner-approved across products/brands. Mappings do not need a separate account selector or duplicated credential reference; `provider_code` resolves the one protected provider configuration. Actual account identity remains in the paid request snapshot and must resolve to the same account after credential rotation. Exact config persistence is still under review; multi-account support alone does not justify another table. Do not parse a composite product/variant code as the relational identity. The relation is **owner-approved table 27**, not executable DDL; see [supplier/preorder review](contracts/supplier-provider-matrix.md).

**Binding at accepted payment is owner-approved.** At committed wallet debit, verified crypto accepted for the live order, or admin-approved bank payment, freeze the then-current shared source/account/product/variant with paid state and durable procurement work in one local transaction; serialize source edits against binding. No remote supplier call occurs inside that transaction. Unpaid order creation/invoice/receipt submission does not bind; the customer quote remains unchanged. Retries and later admin switches never redirect accepted requests. If the selected source is disabled, missing or known unable to supply, retain the accepted payment and paid/unfulfilled administrator-review obligation, recording a typed blocker and any known selected identity without inventing a usable source or runnable purchase. Source re-enablement alone does not bypass staff review. Later unavailable-source discovery retains the original binding. No automatic fallback, credit, lowest-price routing or split purchase. Source blocker/snapshot placement and typed zero-due acceptance fields remain pre-DDL design gates; FR-073 resolves automatic coupon-covered supplier procurement; existing late-payment wallet recovery is unchanged.

Local preorders are fully paid product obligations using existing `orders` and per-unit `fulfillments`, distinct from prepaid coupon issuance. Candidate typed order fields are immutable `is_preorder` and `payment_confirmed_at`; the paid queue is ordered by `(payment_confirmed_at,order_id)` per shared product across brands. Queue position and remaining quantity derive from authoritative paid orders and per-unit states; do not store a second independent position/count authority. Serialize checkout and all allocators for the same product. New inventory writes durable drain work atomically with the stock addition; workers allocate the oldest eligible paid order's complete quantity atomically only when enough stock exists, otherwise assign/reserve none and block newer/smaller orders. Persist one complete logical manifest and recoverable transport work. Never send supplier shortfalls through this queue.

A zero-stock paid preorder is valid and may allocate later, including wallet-funded or verified crypto/bank payment; immediate all-or-nothing/no-stock credit rules apply only to immediate orders. Unpaid preorders still use the existing payment deadline; paid remainders do not expire or receive automatic shortage compensation. Use one stable complete-order manifest with per-unit/message identities and resumable transport, without storing credentials in transport logs. New target orders do not require multiple business delivery batches or split coupon accounting: hold all uses while waiting, redeem all exactly once at complete allocation/fulfillment. Exact approved admin hold/cancellation state representation under FR-079, full-manifest access guards and historical partial-order reconciliation remain gates; do not inherit staging refund workflows.

Staff use current support/admin facilities to decide manually. Support status/text is not an order-state command: opening/replying/closing a ticket does not pause or reorder the queue, cancel the order, release coupons or move money.

Only a separate authorized audited order action can alter the order under reviewed rules, serialized against allocation. Preserve accepted payment and original obligation history; no external refund record/proof is introduced. For an approved paid local-preorder cancellation, the administrator handles repayment outside the shop. The cancellation creates no wallet credit or reversal of the original wallet debit, no payout execution and no external payout amount/reference/receipt/status record. Keep original accepted payments, debits and obligation history; audit the authorized order-state action with actor/reason, without asserting or proving repayment completion. Support activity and unknown outside actions remain non-authoritative. All still-held uses are released atomically and exactly once with approved cancellation under ADR-44, without changing coupon terms or expiry. Exact admin cancellation state/hold transitions remain under technical review. This does not alter independently approved late-crypto or immediate-sale shortage wallet compensation. FR-070 / SC-039; 28 tables unchanged.

Approving cancellation of a paid local preorder MUST atomically release **all** of that order's still-held coupon uses (`held → released`) exactly once with the authorized cancellation and actor/reason audit. No separate coupon action or proof of outside repayment is required. Preserve the claim/history and original coupon expiry, activation, eligibility, limits, actual redeemed count and usage-adjustment values; do not create a replacement coupon, renew an expired campaign or increase its cap. Returned capacity is usable only under the coupon's current ordinary eligibility and limits. Serialize cancellation with complete-order allocation/fulfillment: only one outcome commits; a stale/replayed cancellation cannot release already redeemed uses or permit post-cancellation allocation. Reuse `discount_uses` status transitions and existing audit/jobs; no return-counter, refund or coupon-release table is added.

## Automatic discounts and current FX

`discounts` and `discount_uses` are required. The existing source has one code with `max_uses/current_uses` (which may represent many prepaid units), product- and user-specific applicability and automatic best-discount selection. The redesign **removes partial coverage**: if the selected discount has 10 units remaining and quantity is 11, **reject the quantity entry**, display the allowed maximum and request correction **without creating a failed order**. ## State machines

### Immediate local wallet purchase (non-preorder)

`quoted → confirming → completed+allocated+debited → delivery_pending → delivered`  
or `confirming → rejected_out_of_stock` with **no debit**,  
or `confirming → rejected_insufficient_funds` with **no allocation**.

`confirming → completed` is one DB transaction: all units of the order allocate (or none), order/per-unit fulfillment changes and one-use coupon redemption commit together. If the net price is positive, the correct wallet is debited with an immutable ledger entry; if it is **zero** due to a valid 100% code, no wallet row or debit is required. A failed/no-stock transaction does not consume a code. Telegram delivery is not part of this transaction.

### Coupon issuance and redemption

**Owner-approved single-code prepaid issuance**: an advance purchase of `N` future product units issues **one** brand- and original-member-bound 100%-off product-specific code with `max_total_uses=N` and `max_uses_per_user=N`, paid source reference and default no expiry. The same code redeems across later purchases of any positive unit quantity within its remaining capacity. Prepaid entitlements, goodwill codes and marketing promotions have distinct provenance. The bot automatically applies the best applicable eligible discount; there is no customer opt-out. Every selected discount must cover the **whole accepted quantity**; if the entered value is too high, keep the user on quantity selection with an explicit max and create no order. Redemption history is in `discount_uses`; both coupon usage and inventory allocation are protected against replay/races. Issued prepaid units remain outstanding obligations, not cash refunds or allocated stock. Legacy individual codes must still be migratable losslessly.

**External-payment coupon claims (owner approved)**: Bank/crypto orders insert a `discount_uses(status='held')` row at successful order creation, under a serialized coupon lock. At creation, **new** holds must fit current effective remaining cap. `discounts.redeemed_count` counts actual fully redeemed units, while signed `discounts.usage_adjustment_units` lets the owner set a different effective used count without changing redemption records; effective used = `max(0,redeemed_count + usage_adjustment_units)`. New available uses = `max(0,max_total_uses - effective_used - existing held units)` (also enforcing the current per-user cap). Owner edits may make old holds exceed a newly reduced cap; retain those holds and block further claims. No local stock is held. Expiry or successful replacement cancellation marks the old claim `released`; timely bank/crypto evidence preserves the claim through review/confirmation, and accepted bank payment awaiting restock keeps it until delivery or audited resolution. A successfully fulfilled order changes its hold to `redeemed` with matching `redeemed_count` update. **For an immediate (non-preorder) order, if verified crypto payment cannot allocate any local stock and its full frozen USD-valued quote is compensated into that brand's USD wallet, its coupon hold becomes `released` in the very same transaction as the exactly-once wallet credit**, with no redeemed counter increase or allocated units. A 100%-free or wallet-funded immediate local-stock checkout redeems atomically with stock allocation and, if applicable, debit. No new table: add one typed `status` to existing `discount_uses`. **Approved expiration rule**: releasing a campaign use never extends `discounts.ends_at` or creates a replacement, so an already expired campaign is still ineligible for new orders. An order's validly established hold remains honored on that same protected order beyond campaign expiry. Prepaid paid-value entitlements default to `ends_at IS NULL`, with no silent expiration while liability remains outstanding. **Prepaid account binding and single multi-use issuance are approved; 10/50/100% codes all reject too-large quantity entries.** An owner may explicitly edit percent-off, unit limits, effective used count, validity dates, and activation after issuance without the former limit floor or prepaid-settlement gate. New quotes see edits; old order quotes/`discount_uses` history never change. A listing with an independent positive USDT price supports USDT payment **even if its toman price is fixed**. Customer wallets remain **USD-only**.

### External-payment deadline and Telegram message edit

`created_awaiting_payment → (30 minutes without timely evidence) → expired` is a conditional DB transition scheduled durably in `jobs`. The payment instruction prominently warns "Only valid for 30 minutes" and includes `orders.payment_due_at`. The Telegram payment message ID is stored in `telegram_deliveries`, and after expiry the worker creates an edit intent targeting that message, marking it **Failed — payment window expired**. Retrying a failed Telegram edit does not reopen an order. Wallet/free orders settle immediately with no pending-payment timeout.

`awaiting_payment → under_review` on timely manual receipt submission, or `awaiting_payment → confirmation_pending` when qualifying crypto evidence appears before expiry. Review/confirmation can last beyond 30 minutes, and a confirmed payment with unavailable stock can remain pending restock indefinitely. A fully verified full-quote late USDT payment credits the originating brand's USD wallet once without reopening the original order; late bank transfers and provider anomalies remain staff-reconciliation obligations.

**One actively unpaid order per membership; payment method still immutable**: An order's method/currency cannot change. A successfully created new order for the **same brand membership** atomically cancels a previous no-evidence `awaiting_payment` order, releases its coupon hold and queues an edit of its Telegram payment message to **Cancelled — replaced**, not **Expired**. If new-order validation fails, the original remains pending until its deadline. Enforce a **partial unique index** on `orders(user_id) WHERE payment_status='awaiting_payment'` plus a user lock/serializable transaction to avoid races. A receipt in `under_review`, crypto payment in `confirmation_pending`, or paid/restock order MUST remain intact even if another order is created; only a truly awaiting-payment order is replaceable. Evidence/funds later arriving for a cancelled old order must be reconciled under that original payment; money never migrates to the new order. This **does not imply** all `payments` rows have an order: wallet top-ups remain a separate funding origin.

**Funding vs checkout**: NOWPayments is the only permitted provider for customer wallet top-ups; store credit and wallet history remain USD-denominated and brand-scoped. Crypto order payments also use NOWPayments with a USD-valued quote based on `price_usdt`; bank/card-to-card is a **TOMAN order-settlement path only**, never a wallet top-up or an external USD-valued checkout. Spending an already-funded USD wallet is a separate atomic internal checkout, not a new NOWPayments invoice. `payments` has an exclusive `order_id`/`topup_user_id` origin, and successful funding adds one idempotent USD `wallet_entries` posting; the actual on-chain currency and received quantity remain independently observable. **Only USDT/TRON TRC20 (`USDTTRC20`) and USDT/BNB Smart Chain BEP20 (`USDTBSC`)** can be selected for new crypto invoices; store the selected network-specific provider code on the payment and verify callbacks agree. No other crypto asset, generic USDT ticker, or automatic network substitution is allowed. The current rule limiting top-ups for zero-balance customers is preserved pending specific review. Wallet-topup provider expiry is **not assumed identical** to the 30-minute **order** expiry; verify provider invoice validity and policy separately.

**Shared crypto provider, isolated business records**: All three brands use the **same NOWPayments merchant account and platform-scoped credentials**; there is no provider-account FK or per-brand copy of the secret in `brands`. `payments.provider_payment_ref` must be unique within the common provider account, and the callback must look up exactly one locally created payment before deriving its brand through the order's or top-up user's membership. Brand-specific invoice text is generated at creation but is never authoritative for customer/payment lookup. Distinct brand wallets, orders, customer-facing labels and settlement ledgers remain fully isolated despite shared processor infrastructure. A shared merchant account can expose shared payee identity on hosted provider screens or receipts; acceptance testing must establish the actual disclosure surface.

**Separate TOMAN bank destinations**: The two Iranian brands each have **exactly one active, distinct receiving bank card**. The **last successfully saved** bank destination in that brand's encrypted `brands` fields immediately supersedes the previous card for **new orders**; no active-card list or bank-cards table is necessary. Admin changes use atomic revision checks. `payments` stores immutable encrypted destination-card and account-holder snapshots for each bank order, so replacing the active card does not alter an earlier order's bank instructions, 30-minute validity or receipt audit. A keyed fingerprint detects duplicate assignment without storing a second plaintext PAN. Actual banking-app holder-name disclosure requires operator acceptance; separate numbers do not guarantee public separation.

### External crypto payment

`awaiting_payment/confirmation_pending → verified_paid → allocation_attempt`  
then `allocated → delivery_pending → delivered`,  
or `unavailable → USD_wallet_credit_pending → credited`.

Paid callbacks are idempotent by provider payment identity; credited **USD wallet amount** equals the frozen dollar-valued checkout obligation (numeric 1:1 with a USDT quote by explicit internal policy), not current exchange rates or raw received token quantity. **When no stock can be allocated, atomically set the order to compensated/unfulfilled, credit USD wallet once, release `discount_uses(status='held')` to `released` without redeeming it, and persist customer-notification work.** The customer retains the coupon use for a future eligible purchase, subject to its existing expiration. Failed delivery never repeats credit or coupon release. A late transfer after expiry is not automatically treated as a valid live order.

### Immediate manual bank payment (preorders use FR-065)

`awaiting_payment → receipt_submitted_in_time → review → rejected_unverified`  
or `review → confirmed_paid → allocation_attempt → allocated`,  
or `confirmed_paid → paid_unfulfilled_awaiting_restock` (no timeout) `→ later_fulfilled / manually_resolved`.

External off-platform refunds and their records remain outside AccAbad. Any separately authorized staff update to the recorded order status is audited as a state change, not external settlement evidence; preserve the original confirmed receipt and paid obligation history.

### Supplier API order (separate from local-preorder FIFO)

`payment_confirmed → supplier_request_pending → success / confirmed_failure / unknown / partial_pending_admin`.  
`unknown → reconciliation → success / confirmed_failure / still_unknown`; never treat timeout alone as failure. A five-unit request returning three verified encrypted items stores them as `acquired` (candidate state name), not customer-delivered; all customer release remains blocked and the whole order is paid/unfulfilled in `partial_pending_admin`. No automatic re-purchase, credit, coupon release or external refund. Administrator review and the complete five-unit verified set are required before one whole-order manifest can be released. Each remote operation has its own attempt record; per-unit identity/key scope/dedupe are retained. The entire coupon claim remains held until complete-order fulfillment redeems it once; no new per-unit coupon split. Actual legacy partial deliveries remain a separate migration compatibility gate.

Under approved FR-080, confirmed failure stays in paid-unfulfilled administrator resolution until an explicit safe retry or exactly-once same-brand wallet-credit decision; no automatic credit is performed. **All external refunds are manually performed and recorded outside AccAbad; no `refunds` table, outgoing `payments` kind or refund API is planned.** An authorized order-state change is audited as such, not as proof of any external settlement; historical legacy supplier/preorder refund records are preserved in separately verified read-only archives pending migration signoff.

### Human support and campaigns

`support_open → assigned / waiting_for_staff / waiting_for_customer → closed`, with separate append-only message/reply history and brand-scoped staff actions. This works with AI disabled.

`campaign_draft → audience_materialized → running → completed/cancelled`, with recipient-level `pending/sent/blocked/retry` states on `telegram_deliveries` and durable per-bot throttle/restart recovery. Multiple eligible brand memberships yield distinct delivery rows.

The system cannot claim guaranteed exactly-once Telegram output if the remote send succeeds but its message ID is not persisted before a crash; local commits and idempotency are exactly-once, remote duplicates are best-effort suppressed/reconciled.

### Migrated wallet history

Freeze an authoritative **USD** source balance per `(brand,telegram_id)`, map each source membership to `users.id`, import historical ledger as **non-posting historical evidence**, then record new USD operations only after cutover. Alternatively, replay a complete verified source ledger from a known opening entry and derive the balance; never apply both a source closing balance and historical movements as new credits/debits. **If a source contains genuine non-USD wallet value, cutover blocks for an explicit liability migration policy**; don't silently coerce it into USD.

## Migration and encryption invariants

1. Import Brand A products as master; other catalogs not activated. Preserve order snapshots/obligations for all brands.
2. Empty active stock by deliberate operator authorization; old inventory/credential data cannot disappear from required historical records.
3. Decrypt old protected fields using source-specific legacy keyring and original field prefixes/derivation; re-encrypt required **brand-owned** target fields under the new versioned brand key, and any approved **shared inventory** credentials under a distinct platform-inventory key.
