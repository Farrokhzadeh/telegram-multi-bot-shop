`LegacySourceReader` reads complete records under read-only repeatable-read transactions and the approved schema/migration/constraint/index digest. HMAC commitments cover every field, including receipts, provider payloads, source reviewer identities, promises, delivery parts and supplier/refund evidence. Bounded keyset coverage rejects omitted, extra or changed rows. Inventory coverage excludes unassigned stock; master-catalog coverage selects active, nonremoved products. Unexpected order-line tables stop order import. No target JSON column is added.

`LegacyRecordArchive` encrypts and roundtrips the complete original record. Known encrypted inventory/supplier credentials, submitted service JSON and historical settlement references additionally undergo legacy decrypt, new encryption and decrypt-and-compare. Digests bind identity, key IDs and all ciphertext fields. Original source ciphertext remains recoverable. Callers must durably retain the protected artifacts and frozen manifests before import; the synthetic in-memory archive is a fixture, not a production archive implementation.

`ImportWorkflow.ImportRecordsAsync` verifies source coverage, member coverage, source records and archive evidence before target writes. Each target, its mappings and system audit commit atomically and revalidate on replay. Historical order snapshots retain genuine aliases, original dates, exact quantities/amounts and encrypted original discount metadata. `legacy-net-v1` is an exact historical net projection, not reconstructed current pricing. Wallet orders require the original non-posting debit; zero-value transactions are retained separately. Imported orders stay in credential-recovery review. They create no new debit, stock allocation, supplier request, operational fulfillment, release or customer send.

Payments retain original opaque origins, provider identities, expected amounts, creation/confirmation clocks and source status. Original receipts and source reviewer identities remain encrypted evidence, without invented independent-administrator attribution or IPN signatures. Source-reported settlement posts no second credit. A restricted mapped legacy `manual_deposit` may retain an absent original bank destination; ordinary bank payments still require the destination. Unknown assets, invalid amounts and unsupported origins stop import.

Coupon kind is an explicit protected target projection. Codes use brand encryption and the existing keyed lookup format. Source `max_uses` is retained; source `current_uses` is an immutable initial effective-use baseline in `usage_adjustment_units`. Actual new `redeemed_count` starts at zero, with no invented historical redemption rows. Revision zero uses a system import audit and restricted discount/revision mappings. Prepaid classification requires the original owner, product, paid source order and mapped historical payment evidence. Unowned/shared paid codes stop for reconciliation. Missing source issuers/dates are not fabricated; target creation time records import creation. Subsequent ordinary owner edits retain the normal audit/history contract.

Only the approved master brand creates master catalog rows. Stock is not copied. Unsupported rules/media/service representations retain complete encrypted evidence and remain inactive/invisible for review. Other brands do not receive copied active catalogs. Delivery, preorder, supplier, service and historical refund facts are immutable encrypted audit evidence. They do not assert a new completion, repayment or procurement. Names, language, first/last-seen clocks and inactive/banned member restrictions are retained. A restriction timestamp denotes the manifest-bound observation time, not an invented original ban time. Non-USD liabilities and unrepresentable facts stop import.
