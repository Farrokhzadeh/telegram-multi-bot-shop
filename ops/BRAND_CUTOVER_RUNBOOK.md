# Single-brand cutover runbook

This is an operator procedure, not permission to execute it. The current implementation is qualified only with generated keys, synthetic projections and disposable databases. T063 protected-source validation, T064 full historical commerce import, T066 full reconciliation and T068 isolated-staging rehearsal remain separate gates. T057 real-provider capability gates remain closed. No production/source access, writer stop, live callback handoff, deployment or existing staging mutation is authorized by task completion.

## Required protected evidence before scheduling

Record the single affected source brand and immutable target brand/bot binding; exact source image/commit; deployed schema/catalog and applied migration set; read-only snapshot identity and digest; complete source keyring handles including previous keys; encrypted database/media/response archives and backup digests; source/target resource identities; rollback image/configuration; and independently approved operator/reviewer identities. Store these protected manifests outside the business database/repository. References and hashes identify evidence; never include actual keys, tokens, credentials or connection passwords in this runbook or ordinary reports.

Verify every source-to-target projection against the actual approved copy. Historical user balance and USD denomination must be authoritative; non-USD liabilities block import pending explicit reconciliation. Verify one-product historical order cardinality before dropping order_items. Preserve original quotes, payment/network identities, bank destinations, paid-unfulfilled and uncertain supplier obligations, actual historical partial deliveries, coupon counters/holds and prepaid ownership/source payments, genuine public aliases and external-refund archives. Unknown/shared prepaid ownership is quarantined; no guessed membership or new cash is permitted. Only the designated first-store catalog may become active master catalog; no source stock is automatically imported as saleable stock.

Perform and record a decrypt/restore comparison with generated keys/disposable data first. A real authorized-copy/staging restore drill is a later separate gate, not established by synthetic tests. Verify the target can stop independently on the shared host and that source and target financial, stock and sender writers cannot run concurrently for this brand. Other brands keep their own explicit writer owners and original obligations; they are never a fallback destination.

## Planned freeze and snapshot sequence

1. Obtain the explicit per-brand execution decision after E2–E6 and release prerequisites are satisfied. Bind the exact approved resources and immutable manifests; hard-stop on any identity/digest/schema/key mismatch. Current offline authorization does not satisfy this step.
2. Stop every old writer for the affected brand: bot update consumer, administrator mutations, jobs/timers, fulfillment/delivery workers, imports and alternate scripts. Fence callback/receipt ownership before taking the final snapshot. Verify no old process/lease can mutate money, stock, coupon claims or delivery. Do not infer a freeze from a quiet log or a stopped bot alone.
3. Preserve a protected final database/media/provider-response snapshot, migration list, image/config and all previous-key dependencies. Record the actual freeze time and outstanding IDs without exposing secrets. Inventory can be emptied operationally only through its separate approved owner action; a stock count of zero does not prove paid obligations are reconciled.
4. Reconcile in-flight payments and supplier purchases by original provider identity using separately qualified access. Keep uncertain effects pending; never blindly repay, repurchase, resend or silently release claims. Record the single callback owner and durable handoff watermark in protected evidence. Callbacks arriving during freeze need a protected durable capture/reconciliation procedure; do not drop them or apply them to both systems.
5. Keep source writers fenced while importing to the exact authorized isolated destination. Runtime hosts remain stopped. Verify the frozen schema/source manifest before any source read, and pin every batch to the original immutable source/target identity and digest.

## Import and reconciliation

Import targets and their provenance mappings atomically. Resume from the same immutable manifest and committed fingerprints; changed rows, targets, aliases, source brands or batches conflict rather than overwrite. The derived users.balance.snapshot and users.balance.opening mapping namespaces describe the frozen declared balance projection, not additional physical source tables. Original users and transaction primary keys remain unmodified in their mappings; public order aliases use the distinct checked public_order_reference key kind.

Post one exact USD opening snapshot per membership; preserve historical entries as non-posting evidence. Never combine a frozen opening with posting the historical ledger again. Zero openings retain a frozen mapping without fabricating a zero-value posting. Partial source history does not reconstruct an opening from its sum. Stop on non-USD/negative/unrepresentable liabilities or missing identity, ownership, schema or key evidence. Re-encrypt each approved field to its original target brand/row/field or restricted shared-inventory scope, immediately decrypt-and-compare, and clear temporary byte buffers. Do not export plaintext or admit bonus/imported stock automatically.

Reconcile all records, not only row totals: source/target identities and genuine aliases; exact opening/current balances and non-posting history; original paid obligations and deadlines; supplier attempts/unknown results; coupon effective counters and immutable uses; prepaid source/owner linkage; immutable snapshots; ciphertext/key IDs and equality; media/archive recoverability; complete manifests, saved Telegram anchors and known/uncertain delivery markers. Synthetic wallet-projection reports are not full historical reconciliation and always report releaseReady=false. Any missing/mismatched/unmapped/unrepresentable fact blocks promotion. Preserve evidence and rerun from the same frozen manifest; do not fix counts by deleting records or weakening guards.

## Planned activation and rollback

Only after a separate reviewed release decision may the exact target brand acquire writer/callback/update ownership. Verify old writers remain fenced and that no shared local/supplier unit can be allocated by both systems. Protected uncertain new credential sends retain their explicit recovery state and original manifest; saved-anchor edits follow T034. Starting a process is not proof of reconciled money, delivery or provider compatibility.

Before target activation, rollback means keep target stopped and resume only the verified old writer owner after checking the freeze-window capture. After any target acceptance or external effect, never discard the target database and blindly restart the old snapshot. Freeze both sides, preserve new financial/stock/coupon/delivery/provider evidence, reconcile every accepted obligation and prepare an approved replay/ownership plan before selecting one writer. Other brands retain their original owners and data. Restore may use old ciphertext only while all original key/image/backup dependencies remain recoverable.

Retain old snapshots, keys and images until their dependency manifest, retention horizon, restore proofs, reconciliation and separate owner retirement decision permit review. KeyRetirementGate never deletes a key or grants deletion authority. No actual freeze, source copy, callback handoff, staging rehearsal, activation or rollback was executed by this runbook's implementation.
