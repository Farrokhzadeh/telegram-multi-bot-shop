## Durable purchase and recovery

SupplierContracts supplies bounded, disposable credential/evidence buffers and a redacted provider-neutral result. ISupplierAdapter separates inspection, submission and original-purchase lookup; ISupplierEvidenceArchive requires protected immutable response retention. A stable local intent is passed through every call. Neither an interface capability flag nor a request header establishes a real provider capability; only synthetic adapters are registered in qualification, and normal hosts remain fail closed.

SupplierRequestStore rechecks original brand/member/bot, accepted nonterminal obligation, source revision/enabled state and fixed request binding. Membership/product/order/request locks precede the original job fence. Before a purchase, current exact provider/account/product/variant identity, ready-item eligibility, fresh UTC same-currency cost and an enforceable maximum charge must pass. The inclusive automatic unit ceiling is original cost times 1.10; bound remote quantity supplies the total ceiling, including fees. A changed quote cannot roll the baseline or reprice the customer. Cost approval is an independent current-admin, scoped, content-checked and revision-bound decision on the original cost-limit case, either before submission or after every original submit is positively proven no-effect; it records a separate explicit ceiling and audit without rewriting original cost, source or order terms. A post-attempt cost approval leaves the case retained and queues no purchase; another explicit safe-retry decision is required.

Every submit/lookup/export attempt commits before its remote call. A deterministic job/claim attempt UUID identifies the exact durable intent; one initial submit or exact audited safe retry is permitted. Request state changes do not create another purchase. Calls and evidence archiving run outside transactions. Receipt capture completes the original current unexpired lease in the same transaction as finalization/acquisition/state/outbox writes, preserving the pending-request guard on runnable submit updates. Stale/expired receipts cannot authorize new goods or money. Protected evidence and recovery lookup preserve ambiguous outcomes.

Expired leases with no durable attempt may reclaim the same local job. Expired started work finalizes unknown, retires the lease and enqueues read-only original lookup, without submission. The crash qualification uses an explicitly configured short real lease and actual elapsed time, not a fake database clock or relaxed fence. Unknown/processing responses without acquired subsets keep lookup work; no remote reference permits intent lookup only through an explicitly proven adapter capability. A matching full recovery clears only the transient provider-unknown review. Partial acquisition, identity mismatch, cost or credential-recovery review remains protected. Known terminal success cannot be downgraded by another in-flight read receipt.

## Acquisition and protected bonus attribution

Response evidence must be archived before item attribution. Stable provider/account/item keys are checked globally across customer fulfillments and admin-held inventory. Reordered recovery reuses existing original content/disposition; changed identities/content, excess new customer identities, cross-request identity collisions and ambiguous extras retain evidence for review. Bounded requested customer units are encrypted under the original brand/fulfillment context. Explicitly verified bonuses use the independent inventory key, typed original supplier request/item provenance and admin_held state. Neither an extra customer slot nor saleable stock is created.

Partial goods stay internal with the whole held coupon and original payment. Full, validated acquisition queues the existing complete-manifest preparer only without a protected review. The preparer retains all T034 complete-order, generation, encryption and coupon redemption checks. Zero-due supplier checkout requires no wallet balance, actual payment/invoice or expiry work; it holds the original claim until complete manifest redemption. The native qualification covers mixed three-local/two-supplier acquisition, one admin bonus and a complete five-unit manifest.

Bonus content access requires a current independently authenticated administrator, original brand/request scope and durable content-checked access audit. Temporary plaintext is cleared and returned through a disposable redacted object. Group membership and ordinary customer manifest/metadata reads confer no access. System review/bonus audits and notices contain metadata only, use the configured trusted private staff chat and reuse purpose-specific notice dispatch. Absence of that trusted configuration does not invent a destination.

## Confirmed failure and decisions

Confirmed-no-effect requires a trusted explicit result, archived original evidence and no acquired units/bonuses or charge. The original attempt remains immutable; a later matching status lookup supplies separate no-effect proof for an original unknown/timeout. The baseline guard requires positive archived proof for every earlier submit before another submit can be created, plus the exact administrator retry authorization. A null marker or generic failed/HTTP outcome is not enough. Existing leased procurement, acquired remote units or unknown effects block terminal resolution.

Confirmed failure records a retained accepted liability and staff case, with no automatic retry, compensation, cancellation, inferred outside repayment or external refund record. OrderAdministrationStore remains the single explicit resolution writer. Retry preserves original source/account/remainder/cost/approved ceiling; credit uses original discounted net USD for wallet/crypto or explicit positive administrator-confirmed USD for bank. One typed ledger/audit/resolution/full held-coupon release commits once; local acquired units remain admin-only. Zero-due cases cannot manufacture cash. A race between retry and credit yields one outcome. Cost approvals remain linked across later request revisions only while their exact approved ceiling/audit tuple is unchanged; a replacement approval must authorize its own exact new revision.
