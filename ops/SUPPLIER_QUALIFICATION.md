A post-attempt cost approval leaves the case retained and queues no purchase; another explicit safe-retry decision is required.

Every submit/lookup/export attempt commits before its remote call. ## Confirmed failure and decisions

Confirmed-no-effect requires a trusted explicit result, archived original evidence and no acquired units/bonuses or charge. The original attempt remains immutable; a later matching status lookup supplies separate no-effect proof for an original unknown/timeout. The baseline guard requires positive archived proof for every earlier submit before another submit can be created, plus the exact administrator retry authorization. A null marker or generic failed/HTTP outcome is not enough. Existing leased procurement, acquired remote units or unknown effects block terminal resolution.

Confirmed failure records a retained accepted liability and staff case, with no automatic retry, compensation, cancellation, inferred outside repayment or external refund record. OrderAdministrationStore remains the single explicit resolution writer. Retry preserves original source/account/remainder/cost/approved ceiling; credit uses original discounted net USD for wallet/crypto or explicit positive administrator-confirmed USD for bank. One typed ledger/audit/resolution/full held-coupon release commits once; local acquired units remain admin-only. Zero-due cases cannot manufacture cash. A race between retry and credit yields one outcome. Cost approvals remain linked across later request revisions only while their exact approved ceiling/audit tuple is unchanged; a replacement approval must authorize its own exact new revision.
